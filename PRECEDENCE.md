# Precedence and changes

1. Applicable platform/tool constraints and explicit current operator authorization define what a session may do.
2. A scoped operator-approved work package governs the assigned implementation or research activity. An upload authorization is not an approval of every proposal uploaded.
3. Reviewed decision records in `coordination/DECISIONS.md` identify accepted design direction and separately record operator approval. A recommendation remains a recommendation until that approval is recorded.
4. The cumulative starter preserves retained requirements and the original Generation-0 milestones. New approved revisions must explicitly map every affected requirement and contract.
5. R02/R06/R08 and future handbacks are proposals until reviewed. Newer timestamps alone do not resolve conflicts.
6. Original archive instructions, source examples and test-double schemas are historical inputs. They cannot enable physical/network flags or create production grants.

The root hub protocol supplements navigation and collaboration without rewriting the imported baseline. If an imported instruction would trigger a new coding run, stop and obtain the scoped assignment instead.

Research-thread R03 belongs to ownership H04; R06 to H08; R08 to H09; R13 to H06. Preserve this mapping.

Publication review, design acceptance, implementation authorization, executed acceptance and physical qualification are separate gates. No gate is implied by the presence of a file on main.
