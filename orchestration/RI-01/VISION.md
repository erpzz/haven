# Haven cumulative vision — RI-01 guardrails and opportunity map

This is a new campaign interpretation of explicit user requirements, not a replacement for the original requirement register. Preserve recovered original REQ/AT/EX IDs. The labels V01–V16 below are campaign coverage anchors only; never manufacture missing original IDs.

## North star
Haven is a personalized, distributed, multimodal intelligence system that helps people understand and act in their digital and physical surroundings. Its personal-assistant role remains important, but its vision includes a shared observatory, new senses, scientific exploration, protective robotics, resilient communications and measured autonomous engineering. Follow-ups add capabilities; they do not reset the project around the latest specialty.

## Vision-review questions
For every anchor, name current evidence, owner, near-term useful behavior, ambitious target, next experiment and any temporary deferral. Deferral needs a reason and re-entry condition. Do not delete a target because a first prototype cannot deliver it. Conversely, the user's excitement is not evidence that a hardware/API/medical capability already exists.

Require at least five vivid integrated journeys: a useful everyday request; an image-grounded question with follow-up; a recorded multi-sensor incident replay; a permitted public regional observation in Observatory; and an engineering campaign that learns from a failed candidate. Preserve later silent/cooperative interfaces and qualified physical observation. Real medical diagnosis, unlimited surveillance, unsafe autonomous physical action and imagined perception are not necessary to fulfill this vision.

## Anti-drift tests
Reject narrowing Haven into only a chatbot, drone dashboard, map skin, RMM tool, RF detector, permission engine or CAD generator. Reject rebuilding the whole project around a framework chosen for convenience. Also reject endless architecture/mock iterations with no proposed small path to useful measured behavior. Favor modular interfaces, actual evidence, honest uncertainty and incremental capability growth.

The agent-team structure in RI-01 is development organization. It does not commit Haven itself to ten services, ten models, or an agent per sensor. Integrate with existing owners rather than minting a new central authority for every research branch.
