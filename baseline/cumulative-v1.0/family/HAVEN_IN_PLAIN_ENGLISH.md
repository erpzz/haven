# Haven, in plain English
## A personal assistant that could eventually help in the physical world

We are building Haven: a personal assistant for both of us that can help organize our days, remember decisions, find information and take care of approved tasks. Each of us controls our own private information and what we choose to share.

Over time, it could connect to our phones, glasses and Watches, then to useful sensors, small robots and drones. That could mean finding something we misplaced, explaining what we are looking at, bringing a phone or light, or helping us communicate useful information during an emergency. It should remain helpful even when a drone is unavailable.

The more experimental goal is to help it understand spaces beyond what a camera can see, using wireless signals and other measurements. We would start with detecting presence and movement, then investigate better location and mapping. It must show when it is uncertain rather than invent a convincing picture.

We also want an engineering assistant that helps design useful tools, makes parts with a 3D printer, tests whether they work and improves them. The exciting part is the connection: the assistant could notice a practical problem, help develop a tool to solve it and learn from the actual result.

**The potential payoff is less everyday mental load, more independence, and better information and coordination when something goes wrong.** What could make Haven distinctive is bringing these abilities together around our needs—not claiming that every individual technology is new.

We are building this in small, tested steps. Today it is a project, not a finished rescue system. Detailed through-wall mapping, autonomous fabrication and emergency robotics remain research goals—not promised abilities. Haven would support people and existing emergency services, not replace them.

*PROJECT ASTRA is the platform. Haven is the assistant we would talk to.*
