## Bill-of-materials structure

For every proposed purchase capture exact SKU/revision, official interface/datasheet, complete kit contents, software/firmware/support horizon, controller/host requirements, additional cables/power/spares, shipping/tax, maintenance/consumables, privacy/region restrictions, returnability, and the single experiment it enables. Mark price date and stock separately. Candidate, selected, ordered, owned and qualified are different states.

| Branch | Smallest useful equipment category | Buy only after |
|---|---|---|
| RF presence/localization | Documented CSI/ranging hardware plus repeatable fixtures | Data-access method, allowed operation and baseline experiment selected |
| Geometry research | Suitable measured scanning arrangement and reference targets | Earlier acquisition quality and observability review; no arbitrary phone promise |
| Handheld sensor | One or two justified modules, housing, power and phone interface | Specific question/calibration and data grant defined |
| Printer/workshop | Reliable documented filament platform plus safe workspace and metrology | Three intended parts, ventilation/power/material plan, shared-space agreement |
| Ground robot | Complete low-force development platform and stop equipment | Safe operating area, teleop/skill tests and full BOM |
| Aircraft | Qualified affordable camera or development platform | Exact controller/SDK/firmware/OS and manual recovery evidence |
| Lighting/speaker | Ground-qualified module before aircraft payload | Thermal/energy/glare/intelligibility and attachment review |
| Emergency communications | Ground local network/compute and suitable power; optional backhaul | Offline portal use case and guest isolation; real upstream need |
| Always-on operation | Modest local gateway and measured backup power | Required availability measured against sleeping-desktop limitations |
| Dock/fleet/specialized payload | Compatible supported equipment | Single-device and concurrent simulation acceptance plus site review |

## Historical budget alternatives retained

The earlier research compared the $759 Mini 4 Pro reference with a then-listed $349 Mini 3 kit, a suggested used-Tello target around $100–150, a $249 educational CoDrone, and a Crazyflie aircraft/accessory estimate. These figures are preserved as **historical discussion**, not refreshed quotations or availability. The full archived hardware note has provenance. Recheck exact configurations before using any number to budget.

A cheap aircraft can require a bridge phone, charger, replacement batteries or unsupported integration work. A printable robot needs actuators, electronics, power and calibration. A printer needs usable location, ventilation, material and measurement tools. A free model still consumes compute and operator time. Evaluate total cost per qualified capability, not lowest sticker price.

## Sensor payload budget

Before attaching anything to a carrier, close mass, power, center-of-gravity, mounting retention, vibration, RF interference, heat, weather and useful endurance questions. A low-mass module is not automatically a safe payload. A modified housing may invalidate sensor calibration. No user-owned EC120 attachment is assumed qualified.
