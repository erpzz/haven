# Research ambition with evidence

The target is a genuinely distinctive assistant and engineering system, not a rebranding exercise. Existing research provides foundations and counterexamples. We should attempt novel work where a specific hypothesis and measurement can establish a contribution, while being clear about uncertainty.

## Candidate contributions to investigate

1. Budget-aware active perception that selects between RF, direct-view imagery and historical maps while retaining uncertainty and privacy.
2. A household-scale engineering loop that improves sensor fixtures from measured data without changing its own qualification criteria.
3. Personal/household memory joined to physical evidence without conflating observations, inferences and permissions.
4. A model-economics router that minimizes cost per accepted two-user task while enforcing privacy and physical authority outside inference.
5. Honest store-and-forward human-help communication coordinated with—but not dependent on—mobile sensing.
6. A reusable qualification contract that lets different affordable embodiments supply equivalent evidence without pretending they have equivalent flight or sensing capabilities.

These are proposed research questions, not established novel inventions. Prior-art review must include papers, code, datasets, standards and deployed systems. “Not found in the first search” is not proof of novelty. Patentability and freedom to operate are separate legal questions not resolved here.

## Evidence ladder

IDEA → PHYSICS/INTERFACE PLAUSIBILITY → REPRODUCED BASELINE → CONTROLLED BENCH RESULT → HELD-OUT GENERALIZATION → SUPERVISED FIELD RESULT → OPERATIONALLY QUALIFIED CAPABILITY.

Record evidence at its actual level. A vendor demo, preprint abstract, simulation or anecdote can be useful but supports a different claim. R15 illustrates why later corrections matter; its revised novelty and identification treatment must travel with any use of the original result. Do not repeat compelling headline numbers merely because an earlier assistant used them.

## Experiment protocol

Define the question, baseline, independent variables, apparatus and software hashes, permissions/hazards, measurement method/uncertainty, inclusion/exclusion rules, dataset split, maximum trials/time/spend, decision thresholds and stop/recovery. Record results with the same format for positive, negative and inconclusive outcomes.

Avoid leakage: adjacent frames from the same room/session should not appear in both training and final evaluation if the claim concerns unseen environments. Do not optimize on the final acceptance set. Multiple comparisons and repeated searches require a cautious interpretation; an exploratory result should be labeled exploratory.

An analysis can report uncertainty intervals only when their assumptions and computation are defined. A neural confidence score is not a calibrated error bar. If sample size is inadequate for a strong conclusion, report observed effects and limitations rather than invent precision.

## Human and machine review

The optimizing agent may not own the only test, select only flattering data or rewrite the hypothesis after seeing outcomes without recording the change. A separate reviewer checks provenance, method and alternate explanations. Numerical scripts and raw readings matter more than generated prose. Qualified physical assessment can require human expertise beyond a second model's opinion.

## Reproducibility bundle

Each accepted result includes source/license references, exact artifacts/environment, fixture drawings and calibration, consent-safe input data or a lawful access recipe, commands/configuration, raw logs, analysis and plots, failed attempts, limitations and a reproduction checklist. Public sharing is opt-in and removes private household/device/health details.

## Stopping and pivoting

A time-boxed research failure does not erase the original target. Record what was learned and the next viable measurement/interface change. Keep a capability as deferred/unverified rather than pretending a weaker substitute completes it. For example, successful presence sensing is a valid milestone while geometry remains open.

The research agent can propose new branches, but cannot turn every interesting paper into an immediate build dependency. Score proposals by potential utility, evidence maturity, testability, cost, risk and relation to existing requirements. Avoid blanket verdicts based on novelty excitement alone.
