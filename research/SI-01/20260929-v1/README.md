## Central recommendation

Build a human-centered, permission-aware shared model of objects, places, events and intentional tasks. Keep observation, inference, historical memory, simulation and imagination distinct while allowing them to cooperate. Spatial interfaces, sensory augmentation, engineering and embodied agents should extend a person's understanding and agency, not replace them with an opaque system.
