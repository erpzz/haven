# Conditional Access sign-in applied policy MFA evidence reportOnlySuccess coverage

Sign-in evidence describes which Conditional Access policies applied and their results. Policy definitions alone cannot prove coverage for all users, applications, conditions, exclusions, or actual sign-ins. Report-only results are distinct from enforced policy results.
