# Conditional Access policy state report-only MFA enabled disabled

A Conditional Access policy has enabled, disabled, or enabledForReportingButNotEnforced state. Report-only and disabled policies do not enforce their grant requirements. A display name mentioning MFA does not establish enforcement.
