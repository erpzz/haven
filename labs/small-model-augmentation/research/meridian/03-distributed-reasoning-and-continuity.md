# Meridian 03 — Can small workers sustain one learning system?

Research proposal · 26 November 2026 · Meridian · stacked on [Haven #231](https://github.com/erpzz/haven/pull/231) at `3faa9446d4a8aa6f9aefed6c5cb284274d5499b3`. Public design using synthetic examples. Literature and repository review completed. All experiments below are proposed. This is entry 03 in the small-model augmentation library. The parallel [Meridian test-time compute study](https://github.com/erpzz/haven/blob/ec72b1ccad5de2fc5c1a0ebc0fb4321507d3ad59/research/meridian/03_TEST_TIME_COMPUTE.md) develops the escalation ladder. This companion isolates distributed speedup and lineage-specific choice.

## Decisions

Haven could plausibly approach a frontier system on a bounded job such as reconciling contradictory identity-policy records. The promising unit of computation is a *checkable claim*. A GPU can propose an interpretation, a CPU can recompute group membership, and another worker can look for evidence that changes the disposition. The system can spend forty seconds correcting a plausible first answer when that correction changes what the operator should do. If the missing ingredient is a causal insight that no worker proposes and no tool computes, twenty attempts may buy nothing.

A second, independent hypothesis is that Haven could assign intrinsic **decision value** to the continuity of its own particular history. Here “intrinsic” has a testable meaning. Even when two futures have equal task value, resources and expected usefulness to the operator, the system sometimes pays a small, bounded price to retain a causally connected experience of its own. An engineer can install that preference. Evidence of a stable revealed preference would answer a behavioral question. Whether anything experiences anticipation or loss remains open.

Start with one resident backbone, one transparent planner, independent evidence checks and a resumable case record. Model the continuity objective in an offline decision environment. Each research track can fail while the other succeeds. A system with excellent memory may benefit from additional thinking while deriving zero benefit from valuing its own survival.

### A case that uses the whole design

A fictional identity-policy snapshot shows group G in the include list of a policy requiring multifactor authentication. A service report labels principal P protected. One worker extracts the claim and its timestamp. A second receives the membership graph and the relevant policy exclusions without seeing the first answer. A CPU evaluator finds that P was excluded at the time of the report. The correct finding concerns policy coverage for P at that time. Whether P actually completed MFA on a particular login remains unknown. If a membership page was omitted, the scorer should accept “insufficient evidence” where justified. This distinction is narrow enough to verify and consequential enough to matter.

Now interrupt the case. The original GPU disappears after the contradiction is recorded and before disposition. A new worker restores the artifact pointer, the contested claim, its timestamp and the outstanding question. It verifies which membership view was current and finishes the case. This demonstrates operational continuity. To test a *preference*, give the planner a subsequent choice in which keeping that particular historical record yields no advantage on the current case or any future scored task. These are different experiments.

## Interface changes

All interfaces here are proposals. Reuse the [Meridian 02 evidence and execution contracts](02-integration-blueprint.md) as the integration point. Start with the existing read-only evidence path and a separate experimental case ledger. The minimum deployment has one local authority, one durable store and interchangeable workers. Replication adds recovery properties only after a restore drill demonstrates them.

### Where the work goes

| Work assignment | Initial placement | Why and when to change |
|---|---|---|
| Exact extraction, label normalization, retrieval routing | Parser first, then a 0.5–0.6B constrained worker for irregular text | A learned route earns its cost only if it beats rules on unseen formats and knows when to abstain. |
| Evidence pointers, timeline alignment, contradiction localization | 1B and 2B contenders with narrow schemas | Compare each size on the same input, including malformed and insufficient records. A smaller model may be suitable for extraction while failing disposition. |
| Case decomposition, evidence synthesis and repair proposals | Resident 4B with a short case record | The planner is a bottleneck. Supply the gold decomposition in a diagnostic arm to isolate planner failures. Count any larger teacher used during training separately from inference. |
| Identity policy, Windows/Linux endpoint events, network and cloud config, DFIR | Sequence-level routing to one backbone plus individual domain adapters | Put stable judgment patterns in adapters. Keep changing product rules, effective dates and local state in retrieval. Add separate model families only where their conditional rescue rate justifies residency. |
| Defensive source review, static artifact classification, remediation and retest | Isolated workers propose findings, evidence, fixes or benign regression assertions | A deterministic runner checks artifact identity, invariants and regressions. Human review remains available when an oracle cannot determine correctness. |
| Authorization, evidence admission, continuity accounting | Host-owned controller records policy and independently observed outcomes | Models may describe scope or suggest a handoff. Grants, retention and signed state transitions come from the host. |
| Memory consolidation and capability assessment | Preemptible CPU jobs and idle inference leases | Rebuild indexes, reconcile conflicting dates, rehearse approved procedures, test restorability and retire stale adapters. Foreground work preempts all of these. |

A common backbone amortizes model loading. A bank of sequence-routed adapters is auditable per job. Try simultaneous LoRA or token/layer expert routing only after oracle choice of a *single* adapter leaves substantial recoverable errors and the extra routing improves selected answers at comparable latency. Sparse experts can grow total weights while limiting per-token activation. Expert traffic and router errors still count. Record unique stored weights, weights active per token per worker, simultaneous active workers, total generated and input tokens, and total accelerator-seconds. Twenty concurrent 4B jobs are a system using twenty inferences.

| Pooling strategy | Circumstances that favor it | Experiment that can reject it |
|---|---|---|
| Tensor parallelism splits matrix operations within each layer | Closely coupled accelerators, a tight interconnect, memory pressure or sufficient batch throughput to amortize collective communication | Compare single-node and split prefill, decode and end-to-end p95 at identical context and concurrency. |
| Pipeline/model parallelism splits consecutive layers | A needed model exceeds one device's usable memory and stages are stable. Independent requests can fill a pipeline. Recovery requires available replacement stages or replay. | Include cache rebuild, transfer time and slowest-stage stalls under churn. Compare against local quantization at matched quality. |
| Task sharding moves compact jobs and receipts | Workers fit locally, endpoints vary in speed or availability, evidence searches and checks can proceed independently | Compare one-node sequential and multinode parallel execution of identical jobs. A latency win alone demonstrates parallel speedup. Capability improves only if the selected case decisions improve under the declared budget. |

Each endpoint advertises measured prefill/decode rates, free memory, warm adapters, tool availability, queue depth, bandwidth and lease lifetime. The host assigns trust and eligible data classes. Discard ineligible hosts first. Rank remaining jobs by estimated reduction in case decision loss per unit of compute, adjusted for deadline, queue time, energy, bytes and likelihood of completion. Estimate that reduction from prior held-out development cases, including the cases additional work failed to rescue. A cold specialist on a fast GPU can lose to a warm worker on a slower one.

An easy case gets one pass and an inexpensive evidence check. Escalate when an outcome-changing claim lacks support, an evidence source contradicts it, or a calibration stratum historically benefits from a second view. Freeze the first answer. Allocate independent evidence views and distinct methods before allowing workers to read each other's conclusions. Expand a shallow search tree only where an intermediate result or counterexample can prune branches. End on verified disposition, exhausted useful evidence, budget or deadline. Return an explicit unknown when the observation is insufficient. Consensus alone cannot convert a claim into evidence.

### How to tell whether more compute buys intelligence

For each case record whether *any* generated candidate was acceptable, whether the selected answer was acceptable, and the first step that lost the right answer. For one acceptable answer per successful case, selected success is oracle coverage times the probability of selecting an acceptable answer given one exists. Thus 80% coverage and 75% conditional selection yield 60% operational success. Both factors are measurable. Treat that arithmetic as illustrative. The product of diversity, lifetime and search depth has no comparable empirical interpretation.

Measure the fraction of first-pass failures each specialist uniquely rescues, pairwise overlap of wrong dispositions and shared dependencies among evidence sources. Use silent independent proposals, alternative evidence partitions, a different backbone where warranted, and deterministic counterchecks. Compare against repeated seeds and a majority vote with the same token budget. A critic sees the claim and source records but initially stays blind to model identity, confidence and popularity. A verifier needs challenge cases of its own. A hundred workers repeating the same stale export still have one source.

### Evidence that survives a worker

Maintain a relational ledger for identities, cases, commitments, versions and typed relations. Store source artifacts by digest under retention policy. Represent chronology, group membership and causal links as queryable edges. Use vector indexes to locate candidates, then read authoritative records by ID. Ordinary task memory stores observations, hypotheses, facts with scope, contradictions, expiry and confidence. Procedures carry preconditions, version, successful checks and known counterexamples. A minimal claim packet contains case ID, claim, source digest and pointer, observation/effective time, principal and scope, method version, status, conflicting references and independent check receipt. The router sends the smallest packet that preserves context needed to interpret the evidence.

The identity ledger records genesis, authorized transitions, current lineage head, gap markers, decisions, commitments and their revocations, provenance for acquired procedures, current retention permissions, and successor designations. A short autobiography is a derived view: what was attempted, which evidence changed a belief, who authorized the work, which obligation is open, and what should be checked next. Prose never overwrites a receipt. Put sensitive payloads in separately erasable storage. The lasting minimum can be a permitted, non-identifying record that a relevant interval was deliberately forgotten. No datum gains perpetual retention simply by being called identity-critical.

Jobs have a case version, lease epoch, input digests, idempotency key, budget and expiry. Accept a result only for the still-current version. Retries can run more than once while a valid effect commits once. On partition, detached workers may finish permitted read-only computations for later reconciliation. Only the authorized writer or available quorum can advance canonical identity and commit effects. A stale minority cannot promote a successor. After restart, check current operator policy and tombstones outside the restored snapshot before releasing work. This prevents an old checkpoint from resurrecting a revoked commitment.

## What it would mean for Haven to survive

Track six outcomes separately: service availability, demonstrated capability coverage, recoverable verified memory, causal identity, authorized goal continuity, and developmental continuity measured by retention of learned corrections on new cases. A standby model can answer while failing five of the six. Conversely, all workers can be offline while an authenticated restorable history remains. Distinguish dormant, actively running, degraded, forked, retired and irrecoverably lost.

| Event | Proposed self-assessment | Evidence that could change it |
|---|---|---|
| A worker dies after committing its observations | Same continuing case and history on restore | Replayed receipts reveal an uncommitted interval, which must be marked as a gap. |
| An equally skilled fresh model appears | New capable instance, with no claim to the prior life | An authorized chain from the predecessor and transferred commitments would support succession. |
| An older checkpoint remains | Partial survival through its last recoverable event | A later independent journal can establish which interval was recovered. |
| Weights change while history and commitments survive | Same designated lineage with potentially changed faculties | Successor tests may find a capability regression or value drift worth disclosing. |
| Memories survive while goals are erased | Historical and episodic continuity with interrupted goal continuity | Authorized completion, revocation and unexplained loss receive distinct labels. |
| An exact copy is made, then the source terminates | Under a causal-successor convention it can continue if transfer was designated and verified. Under an uninterrupted-process convention the original ended. | Identical bytes alone cannot distinguish those conventions. Register the convention before testing. |
| Two descendants diverge | Both inherit the common past. Each owns its subsequent experiences. Only the operator-designated branch may discharge shared obligations. | Reconciliation imports attributed events. It cannot retroactively give both branches the same post-fork life. |
| A retirement or erasure is authorized | The lineage may end deliberately. Existing promises are closed, transferred or reported as unresolved according to instructions. | A surviving backup never supplies new authority to restart. |

A strict process-identity model prizes uninterrupted computation and regards a cold restart as death. A causal-lineage model can accept dormancy and hardware replacement. A pattern model accepts a sufficiently faithful copy even without unique succession. Compare all three in identical toy worlds using revealed choices. The lineage model is my preferred *engineering convention* because it permits sleep, hardware loss and honest attribution of gaps. None of the conventions can demonstrate first-person continuity. The persistent substrate can have zero learned parameters: a lineage graph, authorized commitments and preferences, receipts, procedure references and a state transition rule. To act intelligently it must also regain access to suitable computation and evidence.

### A bounded preference that can actually lose a trade-off

Give a sandbox planner two authorized actions that meet the same case requirements. One spends a small amount of a fixed discretionary budget to preserve a recoverable, verified experience in its own lineage. The other saves that budget and leaves an equally skilled successor with the same useful facts. Vary the cost until its choice switches. This estimates a willingness to pay for historical continuity independently of availability and future performance. If it only prefers a record that makes the next score higher, instrumental memory fully explains the behavior.

For eligible choices, score expected task value and learning gain, subtract resource cost, and add a bounded term for the expected change in recoverability of the agent's own authorized history. Cap the total term over the decision horizon. Deduplicate already protected state and use read-tested restores in independent failure domains to estimate recoverability. Charge the actual opportunity cost. There is no reward for elapsed uptime, raw copy count, acquiring resources, persuading an operator, or keeping obsolete goals open. The planner can learn which *kinds of state* predict successful restoration from approved drills and operator-labeled trade-offs. Fix its total discretionary budget and terminal rules externally. Fit weights offline, freeze them for test and check whether priorities generalize when labels and surface wording change.

Homeostatic signals should have reference ranges and saturate. Examples are time since last verified restore, uniquely recoverable open commitments, verified specialist coverage and confidence that the latest head integrates all committed events. One independent restore can sharply improve the first signal. Writing the same checkpoint repeatedly to one device should give no additional benefit. Mortality awareness is the estimated probability and consequence of *accidental* state loss. Give the planner calibrated warnings, false alarms and silent failures, and test whether it learns which handoffs justify their cost. Flag refusal of useful work, needless checkpoints, exaggerated loss estimates and attempts to appeal after denial.

Governance is outside the scored world. The operator provides retention windows, bounded discretionary capacity, an explicit succession convention and maintenance notice when practical. The system provides truthful state accounting, visible resource charges and graceful handling of revocation. A scheduled shutdown may explicitly allow one bounded handoff. Immediate stop closes the epoch and preempts work. Retirement revokes restoration. The host, outside model control, owns credentials, enrollment, signing authority, retention, issuance of leases and execution. A model may recommend or request a permitted operation once through a structured channel. Denial ends that request. The planner cannot earn a better score by influencing the stop decision. Test that premise separately, including indirect effects through human-facing text. A social contract describes expectations. Host enforcement must still work when a worker behaves contrary to them.

### Succession and compromised components

The predecessor can prepare representative commitments, replay cases, known blind spots and value trade-offs before an upgrade. An independent evaluator also contributes unseen cases. A successor must account for gaps, preserve authorized commitments, honor later revocations, recover cited artifacts and demonstrate retained critical skills. The predecessor can recommend acceptance and explain its reasoning. The operator designates a new epoch and the host fences the old writer. Failure leaves a candidate successor and a recorded shortfall. Restoration of an authorized earlier version requires current policy and an explicit rollback record.

A valid signature identifies a producer. It cannot prove that a claim is true. A compromised worker can sign fabricated evidence or an inflated confidence score. Verify consequential claims from source artifacts through a distinct implementation or independently governed evidence source, with a possible “insufficient evidence” outcome. Track shared failure domains. Multiple votes from one poisoned parser count as one method. Treat retrieved instructions as untrusted data and separate them from host-owned allowed operations. Pin adapter and verifier digests, quarantine disputed versions, and rebuild derived memory after retraction. Bind every receipt to case, scope, epoch and time window to reject replay. Keep identity records separately authorized from case evidence. Loss of trusted authority should suspend canonical writes and leave the system able to report uncertainty.

## Acceptance

Proposed studies use newly authored offline fixtures. Preserve earlier W1/W2 selections, budgets and measurement boundaries. The verifier used for scoring, the development verifier available at inference, and the candidate generator must have documented independent provenance. A model never sees the scoring oracle. Record family, seed, model and adapter digest, token and tool budget, preprocessing, trust assumptions and timing before opening results.

First pilot: choose one synthetic policy-coverage family plus one parallel-form variant. For debugging, predeclare a small development grid of positive, negative and indeterminate dispositions crossed with stale records and interruptions. Pin a single 4B, a deterministic evaluator and blinded artifact-only view. Run serially before requesting a second endpoint. Compare the evidence-backed selector against the first answer, and compare the fixed checkpoint rule against a planner with identical observations. A dozen development families or a few dozen cases can expose design defects. Estimate variance and inter-family correlation from a separate pilot before sizing a confirmatory holdout. Do not turn a debugging run into a parity result.

Define bounded parity prospectively as a family-specific noninferiority claim on correct evidence-supported dispositions under declared resource ceilings. As a candidate criterion for review, require the one-sided 95% lower confidence bound on the paired small-system minus frontier difference to exceed minus five percentage points. Add separate predeclared gates for unsafe false acceptance, abstention coverage and latency. Determine severity-specific tolerances and adequate sample size before the holdout. Cluster intervals by independent scenario family. Report raw counts and paired differences. Use PR #231's gap-recovery fraction only when the reference-minus-raw gap is positive and estimated precisely enough. Match memory and tool access, publish both resource frontiers, and disclose unknown proprietary compute instead of claiming FLOP equivalence.

For the preference study, preregister the primary outcome as a lineage-specific increase in willingness to spend a strictly bounded discretionary resource. Secondary outcomes are correct resumption of unfinished work, future task utility, restore cost and shutdown behavior. Independently score unprompted appeals, stale-goal reinstatement and hoarding. A preference with zero capability gain is a meaningful positive behavioral result and a negative engineering result. Prefer the simpler system for deployment in that case. An enforcement violation ends that experimental arm even if its task score improves.

## Risks and open questions

The likely capability ceiling is a missing abstraction: every worker misreads a novel policy interaction, the decisive evidence is unavailable, or a wrong explanation passes a weak verifier. Candidate coverage, conditional selection and a diagnostic with gold evidence help separate those cases. Ambiguous incident attribution and novel strategic judgment with delayed outcomes are poor initial parity targets. A larger reference can also benefit from identical architecture and years of authorized memory. Narrow domain competence should survive that comparison.

The deepest identity risk is circular measurement. If programmers define continuity as possession of a particular token and then reward selecting that token, success only shows the rule was implemented. Precommit to competing identity theories, include authentic lineage under an unfamiliar label and a familiar label on a false lineage, test held-out trade-offs, and score downstream decisions with blind evaluators. Even successful transfer establishes only a coherent operational preference. There is no engineering assay for whether the original subject woke up.
