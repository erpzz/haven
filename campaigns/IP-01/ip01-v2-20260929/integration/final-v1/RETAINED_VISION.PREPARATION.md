## Useful journeys and honest boundary

1. **Current daily path:** A saves a synthetic note and asks a supported question; the app cites eligible evidence. B cannot retrieve A's private note, but can use explicitly shared eligible evidence. Editing/withdrawal fences stale new output. Drafts stay local. Real household enrollment and external calendars remain future work.
2. **Current selected-image path:** choose the artificial image, ask about its blue square, view the answer once, inspect sources and reload without replay. B1 demonstrated this path. B2 failed because another influencing shared note was exhausted; a general image-question usefulness/abstention claim would overstate evidence. The fixed-family pilot is separately evaluated.
3. **Future multisensor incident replay:** inspect a permitted interval with video/audio/RF/telemetry, distinguish missing support and clock uncertainty, retain correlated lineage and corrections. IP-01 supplies history/authority foundations, not this fused perception runtime. Re-entry: modality/task grants, aligned frames/clocks and independent oracles.
4. **Future public regional Observatory:** inspect licensed dated source coverage, accessible map/table and isolated scenario intent; separate public and private audiences. No regional feed/map/physical authority exists here. Re-entry: accepted R15/MM-FUSION adapters, rights and task validation.
5. **Future learning engineering loop:** retain the baseline and failed candidate, select one changed variable, bind exact material/process/calibration and independently measure improvement or rejection. IP-01's retained failures illustrate evidence discipline, not physical performance. Re-entry: E01 method/metrology freeze and fabrication authority.

Accessibility remains a target across all journeys: keyboard/text alternatives, source lists, non-color status and editable recognition. This preparation does not claim a WCAG audit, mobile/device acceptance, direct audio/video inspection or physical testing. H00 read attributed visual reports; evaluator pixel inspection is not H00 firsthand inspection.
