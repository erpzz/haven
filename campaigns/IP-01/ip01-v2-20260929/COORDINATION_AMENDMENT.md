# Operator continuation amendment — external Super Admin check-ins

This records the current operator's mandatory continuation instruction. It supplements WORK_PACKAGE, START_HERE, TASK_GRAPH and PRECEDENCE; it does not rewrite the historical run or grant new operational privileges. Issue20 is the durable communication channel between the executing root and the external ChatGPT project steward / Super Admin. The executing root retains local scheduling, native children, runtime state, publication, deadlines and shutdown. The external steward is an independent project-level review and steering layer.

## Consume guidance and preserve authority

Before substantive dispatch, fetch all current Issue20 comments and identify the newest comment explicitly labeled or clearly functioning as Super Admin / external stewardship guidance. Record its URL/ID and disposition in campaign state, reconcile against the controlling package and PRECEDENCE, and apply bounded corrections already within IP01 scope. The post-reset comment https://github.com/erpzz/haven/issues/20#issuecomment-5898973462 must be explicitly consumed. Any proposed expansion of permissions, budgets, model selection, physical activity, account access, repository privileges or other operator-controlled scope is HUMAN_DECISION_REQUIRED, not authorized by a comment.

## Mandatory outbound boundaries

Post a compact Issue20 checkpoint at every boundary:

1. A: expired prior run reconciled and distinct bounded continuation established.
2. B: exact final candidate bytes, manifest and Git identity reconciled.
3. C: FINAL-EXECUTION has a real PASS, FAIL, INCONCLUSIVE, PARTIAL or BLOCKED disposition.
4. D: before the final H00 integration plus CODE-FINAL, VISION-FINAL and EVIDENCE-FINAL wave.
5. E: after those reviews return and before any final completion claim.
6. F: delivery and shutdown.

Also post when a new blocker threatens completion, an independent reviewer finds a material defect, executable candidate bytes must change after freeze, model/browser/lifecycle evidence contradicts an earlier claim, a required review becomes impossible, or an operator decision is needed. Do not post routine passing tests or minor implementation details. The operator's new mandatory boundaries govern continuation postings; the historical two-of-four runtime checkpoint use remains recorded unchanged.

## Immediate readback and major gates

Immediately after every outbound checkpoint, reread Issue20. Compare against the last consumed stewardship comment; explicitly record any newer URL/ID and disposition. Apply bounded corrective guidance before the next gate where appropriate. This check is required before candidate freeze/refreeze, final deterministic execution, final model evaluation, H00 final synthesis/integration, dispatch of final code/vision/evidence reviewers, declaring IP01 complete, changing PR draft status or proposing merge/promotion.

Do not wait indefinitely for a response. If no newer guidance exists and the next action is authorized, continue. Perform a requested bounded correction or explain why the evidence does not support it. Preserve substantive disagreements and both parties' evidence; do not manufacture agreement.

## Required contents of every checkpoint

- Exact campaign/continuation run ID, branch, exact published commit and exact evaluated candidate revision if different.
- Assignments used/remaining, active native child identities and roles, and current task states.
- Real tests executed since the prior checkpoint; browser/model/lifecycle calls and attempt counts.
- Current resources/deadline usage; what changed, failed and was repaired.
- Author-produced evidence versus independently executed/reviewed evidence.
- Open blockers/watch items and the next one to three admissible actions.
- Whether candidate bytes changed, whether prior evidence still applies, and whether owned processes are running or stopped.
- Direct links to relevant receipts/reviews where possible. Scheduling in tasks.json never proves execution.

## Questions, role separation and closure

For a genuine design/review ambiguity inside existing authority, mark SUPER ADMIN QUESTION and state the exact decision, affected task/contract, evidence, alternatives, work that can continue independently and what remains blocked. Do not ask the steward to approve ordinary authorized edits, tests or repairs.

Steward feedback replaces neither root scheduling, H00 integration, executable QA, final code/vision/evidence review nor operator authority. The steward cannot author and review the same candidate. Agreement is not empirical evidence.

The expired overnight run remains historical: never reset or erase model counts, suite starts, failures, deadlines, review findings, candidate identities or resources. A distinct bounded closure run performs reconciliation, exact candidate identity, affected/missing final execution, H00 integration, independent reviews, delivery and shutdown. No new features, hardware design, SI01, HW01, additional models or unrelated research.

Do not call IP01 complete until the old state is reconciled, exact evaluated bytes are reproducible, final execution has an honest disposition, H00 final integration is frozen, all three independent final reviews have returned, material findings are resolved or explicitly retained as blockers, MORNING_REPORT and RESUME reflect actual evidence, publication is reconciled and owned processes have exact cessation receipts. At completion, post F, then reread Issue20 one final time for newer stewardship before ending the goal. Communication grants neither authority nor evidence.
