## Five integrated journeys

1. **Daily decision recall:** A asks why a sensor stand was selected. Return A's current dated note, expose correction, draft a three-step plan, and share only the plan with B. Measure correctness and useful task completion without private-source leakage. Later reminder delivery comes from scheduler receipts, not prose.
2. **Image-grounded follow-up:** A selects a workbench image and asks which fixture is present. Cite image region and uncertainty. Resolve 'will that fit?' explicitly, then consult dimension records; no scale or hidden geometry inferred from a picture alone. Propose one permitted observation when evidence is missing.
3. **Multisensor replay:** Replay image, RF, telemetry and delayed correction with capture/receipt/processing times and what was known then. B sees only authorized sources. Show uncovered intervals and correlation; old imagery is not current occupancy. A next-view proposal is separate from movement authority; help remains independent.
4. **Public Observatory:** Answer a permitted regional water/weather/terrain question with source age, resolution, rights and gaps. A scenario fork explores relay location without touching live resources. Deliberately share a field pin/private cue. Compare usefulness to a map plus event table, avoiding causal/live-state overclaims.
5. **Engineering learns from failure:** A candidate loses to the measured baseline. Retain failure, keep evaluator fixed, propose bounded revision and independent review. Later authorized fabrication/measurement accepts only held-out improvement. Trace CAD/process/raw observations/result; STL or completed print is not accepted performance.
