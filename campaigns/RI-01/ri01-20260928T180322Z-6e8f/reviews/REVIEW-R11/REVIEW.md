## Independent primary-source assessment

The 23-entry source register was read; load-bearing claims below were independently checked against primary text or primary indexed passages during this assignment. This is not a claim that all 23 pages were fully retrieved. Remote source bytes were not archived or assigned invented SHA values. Access was on 2026-09-28; volatile product terms require a new check before procurement or integration.

- **Protocol meaning:** RFC 9171, January 2022, section 5.7, defines delivery to an application agent without implying processing, human acknowledgment or rescue. RFC 9713, January 2025, updates the administrative-record registry with BP-version applicability; its type-2 custody signal is BPv6, not a new universal BPv7 custody guarantee. R11 preserves this distinction. [RFC 9171](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9171.html), [RFC 9713](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9713.html).
- **Drone study denominator:** Schierbeck et al., 2023, DOI `10.1016/S2589-7500(23)00161-9`, reports 211 alerts, 72 deployments, 58 deliveries, 55 paired timings and 37 drone-first arrivals. The conditional timing result cannot become an all-alert service rate or survival benefit. Primary abstract exclusions include young children, trauma and EMS-witnessed events; weather, darkness and delivery restrictions limit transfer. The primary article's abstract/methods text was inspected, not all figures or the entire paper. No clinical benefit is transferred to Haven. [Primary article PDF](https://hb.diva-portal.org/smash/get/diva2:1819350/FULLTEXT01.pdf).
- **Native fallback:** Apple eligibility depends on supported iPhone, software, sky view, region and service conditions. Google's Pixel eligibility likewise depends on supported model, software/activation, messaging configuration and region; regional listing does not assure coverage. Neither support page qualifies a Haven third-party SOS adapter. The 911.gov guidance also prevents treating an app as universally able to contact emergency services. [Apple](https://support.apple.com/en-us/101573), [Google](https://support.google.com/pixelphone/answer/15254448?hl=en), [911.gov](https://www.911.gov/calling-911/frequently-asked-questions/).
- **Mesh security:** Meshtastic documentation describes unencrypted headers, known default channel keys and channel authentication/integrity limits; newer direct-message protection depends on endpoint versions and key exchange. Encryption alone is neither identity verification nor authorization. [Meshtastic encryption](https://meshtastic.org/docs/overview/encryption/).
- **Costs and power:** Primary Garmin plan passages and Starlink product/support passages are conditional component terms, not an eligible all-in quote. Starlink product/support average-power ranges differ; averages are not startup/peak or measured runtime. DJI accessory figures depend on mode and lab conditions; combined accessory measurements cannot be replaced by adding maximum nameplate powers. No owned EC120 tuple or end-to-end field budget is established. [Garmin plan terms](https://support.garmin.com/et-EE/?faq=8hWxnISl7V4eBdcTSChZY7), [Starlink power](https://starlink.com/vc/support/article/a7fe6525-5356-a837-48e6-33df176cdbc5), [DJI specifications](https://enterprise.dji.com/matrice-4-series/specs).
- **Rights:** ION's current license text requires preserved notices/disclaimers and limits institutional endorsement; Meshtastic firmware's GPLv3 identity is distinct from documentation, hardware and provider rights. Moving branches are not immutable adoption artifacts. The article's CC BY-NC-ND terms do not grant blanket downstream figure or commercial reuse rights.
