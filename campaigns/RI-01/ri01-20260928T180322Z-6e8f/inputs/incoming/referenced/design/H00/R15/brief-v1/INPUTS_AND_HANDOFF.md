## Review gates

- Every claimed current tool/API/license fact has an appropriate primary source and date/version.
- Public, private, shared, historical, inferred and simulated records remain distinct.
- A no-paid-services profile identifies both credential-free and free-key-required dependencies; free software is not zero operating cost.
- The GEV reuse choice includes a concrete API boundary, maintenance path and alternative, not only a screenshot.
- At least 18 test cases have setup, expected result, retained evidence and reviewer; unrun tests remain NOT_EXECUTED.
- At least one small demonstration has measurable utility compared with GEV plus a separate event table.
- No speculative device or sensing ability is presented as implemented; no physical or emergency qualification follows from a UI test.
- Exports contain no private keys, personal health/location history, household recordings or employer records.

## Communication and publication

Use issue #12 for short questions and artifact references when messaging is explicitly authorized. Posting is not required to finish the research; file-only handbacks are acceptable and must say NOT_UPLOADED.
