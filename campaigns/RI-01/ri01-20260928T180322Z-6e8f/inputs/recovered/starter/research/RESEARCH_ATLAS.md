# Research atlas: demonstrations, tools and testable adaptations

These cases cover real field studies, controlled research, accessible development platforms and restricted-access models. They are deliberately not presented as equally mature. The adaptation ideas and metrics are original proposals. No capability is claimed built, novel or clinically effective merely because a related paper exists.

## Additional research leads, not yet verified selections

Perching for persistent observation; tactile/compliant manipulation; event-camera sensing; multimodal walking/flying or surface-contact robots; opt-in shared sensing; accessible haptic navigation; autonomous sensor-placement; energy-aware cooperative robots; qualified long-duration ground communications; automated dimensional metrology; sim-to-real uncertainty estimation. These are search directions, not claims of a specific successful system or approval to buy it.

H12 should select one at a time based on user value, available primary evidence, reproducibility, cost and safety. For each, retrieve the actual paper/code/hardware documentation, check corrections and license, identify what was really demonstrated, then propose a bounded adaptation. Do not let an endless frontier survey block useful existing milestones.
