Protected success/failure criteria and exclusion rules:

Hazards, radio/legal/clinical boundaries and required permissions:
Physical operator, stop control and recovery:
Procedure with approvals before each side-effect class:
Evidence/logging and raw artifact identities:
Analysis method; handling of missing/contradictory data:
Promotion gate and explicit limitations:

Status starts PROPOSED_NOT_EXECUTED. Approval of a document is not proof of a successful experiment. A negative/inconclusive result is a valid handback and remains in history.
