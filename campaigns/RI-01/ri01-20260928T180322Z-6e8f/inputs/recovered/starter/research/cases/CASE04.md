## What to learn

Acquisition metadata and reproducible fixtures are reusable even if the later reconstruction method changes.

## Smallest useful adaptation

Design a permitted node/fixture dataset with separate stationary/no-motion and moved-environment sessions.

## How to evaluate

False alarms per hour, missed-presence cases, sensor drift and setup repeatability.

Declare the baseline, input/fixture permissions, configuration identities, ground truth and independent review before testing. Retain failures and uncertain outcomes. No test is executed by this research card.

## Boundary
