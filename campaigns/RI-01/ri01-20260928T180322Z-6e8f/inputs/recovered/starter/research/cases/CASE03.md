## What to learn

Training supervision and deployment observations can differ; collect ground truth carefully and test domain shift.

## Smallest useful adaptation

Use licensed public/synthetic data to compare presence/localization with pose hypotheses; avoid real subject identification.

## How to evaluate

Held-out subject/session error, ambiguity and abstention.

Declare the baseline, input/fixture permissions, configuration identities, ground truth and independent review before testing. Retain failures and uncertain outcomes. No test is executed by this research card.

## Boundary

Pose estimates are not identity, medical diagnosis or generic WiFi support.
