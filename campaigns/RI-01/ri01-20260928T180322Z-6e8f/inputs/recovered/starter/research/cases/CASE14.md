# CASE14 — Drone AED delivery as a whole response chain

**Primary evidence:** R16 in [source register](../SOURCES.md). **Maturity:** PEER_REVIEWED_REAL_WORLD_OBSERVATIONAL. **Owner:** H11.

Read the source entry for the exact verified finding and access limitations. The following is an original Haven adaptation proposal, not a claim that the cited researchers implemented Haven or that this package reproduced their work.

## What to learn

Useful delivery depends on eligibility, arrival, access, human understanding and communication—not flight speed alone.

## Smallest useful adaptation

Simulate a dummy-kit request and human acknowledgement; model unavailability, inaccessible destination and late arrival.

## How to evaluate

Time to understandable information/access; failed handoffs and independent-help continuity.

Declare the baseline, input/fixture permissions, configuration identities, ground truth and independent review before testing. Retain failures and uncertain outcomes. No test is executed by this research card.

## Boundary

No claim of survival benefit for Haven, treatment advice or real dispatch authority.

A promising result becomes a separately approved work package, not an automatic new runtime tool.
