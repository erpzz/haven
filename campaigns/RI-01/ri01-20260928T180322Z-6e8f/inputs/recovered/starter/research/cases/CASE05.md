# CASE05 — Complementary sensing in degraded spaces

**Primary evidence:** R06 in [source register](../SOURCES.md). **Maturity:** AUTHOR_PREPRINT_WITH_FIELD_CONTEXT. **Owner:** H10.

Read the source entry for the exact verified finding and access limitations. The following is an original Haven adaptation proposal, not a claim that the cited researchers implemented Haven or that this package reproduced their work.

## What to learn

Different sensors fail differently; fusion should expose degraded modes rather than average them into false confidence.

## Smallest useful adaptation

Replay recorded/synthetic visual, depth and motion streams with one corrupted modality at a time.

## How to evaluate

Localization error versus best single modality; time to flag degradation.

Declare the baseline, input/fixture permissions, configuration identities, ground truth and independent review before testing. Retain failures and uncertain outcomes. No test is executed by this research card.

## Boundary

No simulated smoke or missing camera should be treated as qualified real-fire navigation.
