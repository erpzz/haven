# CASE01 — Coordinated radio measurements before flying scanners

**Primary evidence:** R01 in [source register](../SOURCES.md). **Maturity:** CONTROLLED_RESEARCH. **Owner:** H08.

Read the source entry for the exact verified finding and access limitations. The following is an original Haven adaptation proposal, not a claim that the cited researchers implemented Haven or that this package reproduced their work.

## What to learn

Spatially deliberate measurements matter more than simply adding a drone. Separate measurement geometry from transport networking.

## Smallest useful adaptation

Replay a known synthetic target over alternative permitted sample positions. Compare fixed and adaptive selection without flying.

## How to evaluate

Reconstruction error, coverage, number of observations, calibration sensitivity.

Declare the baseline, input/fixture permissions, configuration identities, ground truth and independent review before testing. Retain failures and uncertain outcomes. No test is executed by this research card.

## Boundary

Do not present the study as an instant stock-phone room scanner.
