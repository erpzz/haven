## Changes requiring a decision, not quiet substitution

Removing a target; replacing the runtime/autopilot/model baseline; assuming a newly purchased device; converting personal preferences into authority; changing a partner's data sharing; promoting an experimental result into an operational capability; moving work into the active M0 tree; or opening a public service. Record the proposal, impact and actual operator decision.

## Scope completeness versus implementation completeness

The requirement-to-owner-to-test matrix provides an inspectable coverage check. It is not proof that the written designs solve every engineering problem. Gaps discovered by the next agent must be added with identifiers and ownership rather than hidden or filled with invented compatibility facts.
