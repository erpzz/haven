## Economics and selection rule

For one illustrative later mono request of 30 seconds and 500 charged TTS characters, source rates imply STT $0.008 plus TTS $0.008 = $0.016 before retries, other infrastructure, tax, network/storage and any reasoning model. This is arithmetic from quoted units, not a bill, latency measurement or service recommendation. Use no free-tier assumption in admission reservations. Multi-channel billing, selected regions/SKUs and provider policies must be reconfirmed at execution; historical prices cannot authorize a call.

Local economics sheet must record exact CPU/GPU/RAM available to the granted package, installation/reproducibility effort, cold/warm memory and runtime, Wh, storage/model footprint, update maintenance, operator transcript correction/listening time and success denominator. None was measured here. Existing host capacity is UNKNOWN rather than an invitation to inventory unrelated devices. No purchase recommendation. Candidate source minima are not the complete host budget.

Choose the least costly qualified task profile that meets protected quality/accessibility/privacy thresholds on intended inputs. If no local profile meets them, a hosted/stronger candidate requires fresh privacy eligibility and reserved spend; otherwise return an honest limit or typed-input alternative. More parameters and lower paper WER do not establish correct proper names, recipient intent, cancellation, or calibrated acoustic/video grounding. Record every failed and inconclusive candidate rather than only the winner.
