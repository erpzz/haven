## Decisions

**MF-D1 — Useful selected image first.** One eligible original image, a typed question or selected region, currently permitted supporting notes, and a source-linked answer are the first useful multimodal behavior. A supported color, label or text answer must not wait for calibrated 3D or audio. Keep original M4 `qwen2.5vl:3b` advisory profile unchanged. A generated illustration can explain a concept but never substitute for camera evidence.

**MF-D2 — Fuse typed claims late by default.** Specialized decoders/DSP/CV/geometry produce bounded evidence with original support, errors, dependencies and abstentions. Join only compatible facts needed for the question. Return supported portions alongside conflict and unknown, or propose a permitted additional observation. Do not route arbitrary RF, IMU, EEG or health arrays to a text model and call the resulting prose signal interpretation. Perception scores do not become spatial covariance, authentication or clinical probabilities.

**MF-D3 — Keep early fusion as a qualified alternative.** Joint feature/raw-input models can exploit information discarded by separate estimators, but require exact synchronization, channel/frame conventions, paired-data rights, missing-modality handling and an independently evaluated task. They make influence and correlated failure harder to inspect. A general audiovisual model is therefore a candidate comparator for a specific question, not a universal replacement. The classic multimodal taxonomy supports treating alignment, fusion and representation as distinct problems; our modular-first choice is a proposed engineering decision, not a measured superiority result. [Primary taxonomy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.09406).

**MF-D4 — Reuse one authority and lineage model.** CORE I01–I07 and accepted R07, MM-VISION, MM-AV and R14 profiles remain authoritative. MM-FUSION adds an operation-specific compatibility record and evidence-set composition, not an all-powerful service. Existing application modules, asset storage and SQLite metadata are sufficient candidates for the first slice. Permission-filtered exact/lexical/region/time lookup precedes optional embedding search; embeddings need a demonstrated retrieval benefit and remain sensitive derivatives.

**MF-D5 — Distinguish the output and action.** Text, plots, overlays, private audio and haptics each have an audience and current release/consume eligibility. Initial finite use means ONE_RELEASE_ONE_DESTINATION_WHOLE_OUTPUT. Audio plus captions, shared display plus private earset, continuous chunks and recurring reminders are not free additional destinations or uses. They need separate reviewed profiles/authorizations. Physical proposals go to existing domain controllers; an answer, map or interpreted wearer intent never dispatches motion.

**MF-D6 — Preserve experimental identities and gates.** R07 E07-B remains a separate 20/20/20, eight-candidate proposal, not R06 E0's 20/10/30 with16 candidates.
