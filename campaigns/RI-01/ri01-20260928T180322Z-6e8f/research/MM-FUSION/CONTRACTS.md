# Proposed fusion contracts

Namespace `ri01.proposed.mmfusion.v1`, no deployed schema/API. CORE I01–I07 plus exact R07/MM-VISION/MM-AV/R14 records remain controlling; this file is an additive composition profile. Existing owners resolve their fields. Unsupported/unknown profiles reject explicitly, never infer compatibility from a similar name. Immutable refs carry type/ID/revision/SHA256/canonicalization version; identity hashes grant neither authenticity nor authority.

## F01 — ModalityProfile and minimum evidence

Registry entry binds modality/operation, producer/version, input/output schema and semantics refs, exact adapter/decoder/model/runtime/tokenizer/prompt/preprocessing/quantization, artifact/license/data-policy refs, allowed claim classes, task/environment coverage, calibration and score interpretation, resource/cancel/stop profile and qualification evidence. A profile may support text-only claims without acoustic or metric claims. Unsupported modalities are not silently converted to captions.

EvidenceAtom extends CORE SourceRecord/DerivedEvidence and native modality contract, with:

- Immutable original raw-byte identity separate from canonical metadata digest, origin/authenticity status, source locator (document span/page, original pixel polygon, frame PTS/interval, audio samples/channel, spatial region or native measurement range), retained-original status and exact derivative transforms.
- Producer/profile, native typed payload/units/encoding, original and normalized values with dimension and bounded conversion record, claim kind and support class, observed versus inferred versus historical versus generated/simulated/stylized/reference. These are distinct axes, not one misleading Boolean live flag.
- Original source clock/boot/ticks or sample indices/rational timebase, capture support interval, receipt/process/knowledge times, clock mapping and uncertainty/validity; every omitted/dropped/unobserved span remains explicit.
- Frame/map/CRS/origin/datum/scale/calibration/fixture refs where applicable; statistical uncertainty union UNKNOWN(reason), bound/region, calibrated task score or declared covariance. A raw uncalibrated score remains raw.
- Correlation groups, dependence edges, derivation parents including model priors/track state/uncited influences, conflict and quality/OOD/dropout status; full current CORE authority and retention/deletion dependencies.

No omission in this summary waives a native AV/MV field. A FusionSet references atoms; it does not mutate originals or overwrite incompatible hypotheses. Required source unavailable means ORIGINAL_UNAVAILABLE for claims needing reread; retention rules still govern existing derivatives. Do not retain raw media indefinitely merely to satisfy a design field.

## F02 — Alignment and operation-specific compatibility

AlignmentDecision binds exact atom revisions, requested operation, profile revision, accepted/refused/unknown outcome, normalized support and refusal reasons. There is no global `aligned=true` that licenses all operations. Same-document lookup can work with unknown physical clocks; synchronized causal/metric claims need their own qualified alignment.

Time: preserve integer native ticks, rational bases and units. Use a versioned calibrated mapping such as `t_ref = a*t_source+b`, its drift/offset uncertainty, validity and transformations (resampling/filter delay/edit/trim/padding/exposure). Convert only exactly or under an explicitly permitted recorded rounding bound; reject overflow/nonfinite or unknown conversion. PTS is presentation support, not proven UTC capture; DTS is decode order. Arrival order, file modification time and average FPS cannot establish cross-source event order. RTP source clocks and LSL acquisition timing require actual profile evidence, not merely a library name. [RFC7273](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc7273), [LSL timing](https://labstreaminglayer.readthedocs.io/info/time_synchronization.html).

Represent a conservative event support interval after propagation of measurement/window and clock error. Assert A_BEFORE_B only when A's latest possible support is strictly before B's earliest under the frozen task policy. Overlap or unknown mapping yields ORDER_UNKNOWN; equality is not strict order. Disjoint intervals do not establish causation. A transcript can supply lexical claims but not prosody, speaker identity or unobserved acoustics. Explicitly supported alignment timings remain estimates, not proof the transcription is correct.

Space: retain R07/R06 paired SpatialSemantics ID/hash/version: `p_parent=R*p_child+t`, normalized quaternion xyzw, parent-frame left perturbation SE(3), covariance order `[tx,ty,tz,rx,ry,rz]`, m²/rad²/m·rad, symmetry/PSD validation. Identify frame axes, handedness, map revision, transform path, calibration and scale evidence. Unknown/relative scale cannot become metres. The ROS fixed-axis covariance layout is not sufficient to assert equivalence to this perturbation convention; a reviewed transform/Jacobian is required. [REP103](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/master/rep-0103.rst).

Map/odom identity matters: relocalization can change map estimates discontinuously while odometry drifts. Resolve exact versions rather than joining on `map` alone. Geospatial values require CRS/axis order, coordinate epoch where relevant, horizontal datum, unit, vertical datum AMSL/ELLIPSOID/RELATIVE_HOME/UNKNOWN, geoid/home revision and boot; no home-relative altitude as terrain clearance. RGB crop/resize/undistortion changes pixel-to-ray mapping; preserve intrinsics/extrinsics and transform lineage. Unit conversion cannot supply calibration, metrology or covariance. R08 scalar-only profile remains UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE for full pose, force or sensing-effect claims. [REP105](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/ros-infrastructure/rep/master/rep-0105.rst).

## F03 — Correlation, disagreement and fusion result

DependenceGraph references SAME_CAPTURE, DERIVED_FROM, SHARED_CALIBRATION, SHARED_PRIOR, SAME_ESTIMATOR, TEMPORAL_DEPENDENCE, CROSS_COVARIANCE_KNOWN or DEPENDENCE_UNKNOWN edges with method/evidence. A frame, crop, OCR, caption and VLM answer share one origin. Nearby frames and different model outputs may share errors. Two sources agreeing does not prove independence; unknown dependence forbids independent-weight multiplication/inverse-covariance fusion.

Default algorithmic proposal: (1) resolve current eligible closure, (2) retain compatible support atoms, (3) compare typed claims for overlap/contradiction, (4) select a qualified single estimate or display separate estimates if no joint uncertainty model exists, (5) return supported facts plus explicit unknown/conflict, (6) optionally propose a bounded next observation. No weighted sum of confidence scores is a posterior by default. Later covariance fusion needs a declared joint statistical model, correlation assumptions, Jacobians and held-out calibration tests. No such model is implemented here.

FusionSet fields: immutable query/task, selected atoms and excluded/withheld reasons (audience-safe), alignment/dependence graph refs, supported fact list, alternative/conflict list, unknown extent/interval, method/version and uncertainty meaning, full influence closure, valid/knowledge-time query, budget/attempt state and output profile. Answer/proposal links each load-bearing statement to support, not merely an overall source list. A schema-valid or cited sentence can still be semantically wrong; independent semantic evaluation remains necessary.

Preserve R07 support states OBSERVED_OCCUPIED/OBSERVED_FREE/OCCLUDED/UNOBSERVED/CONFLICT beside estimates. Missing sensor data and RF silence do not clear occupancy. Historical geometry remains historical; no map overlay is a safe-route guarantee. Generated thermal styling, simulation entities and annotations never acquire observed status by being rendered with a timestamp.

## F04 — Scoped retrieval, memory, corrections and restoration

Assets can remain bounded files/object references; SQLite can hold immutable source/derivation refs, eligibility indexes and comparison records. No mandatory embedding store/broker/service is introduced. Begin with exact IDs, metadata, time, region and lexical search. Add embeddings only if a separately frozen relevance/utility comparison justifies them, with exact model/preprocessing/source identity, rights, correction/deletion and leakage evaluation. Similarity is neither identity nor truth.

Use the **complete canonical CORE AuthorityVector** by exact ref: vector_version, authority_instance_epoch/restore_epoch; policy/purpose/rights refs; principal/session/device binding and epoch; service capability/tool catalog; grant revisions/revocation epochs; source revisions/hash/lineage/authenticity epochs; subject/participant-area scope; job/lease/cancel epochs; destination/epoch/audience/route profile; provider policy; closure digest/canonicalization. Local provider policy is explicit NOT_APPLICABLE. Recheck full current equality, operation/purpose, expiry/time uncertainty and complete transitive closure at retrieval, context construction, egress, release and consumption. No cached vector or historical receipt acts as bearer permission.

Filtering must occur before restricted material can influence ranking, summaries, counts or model context. Eligible crops do not erase parent acquisition restrictions. Unauthorized candidate presence/counts/thumbnail/nearest match must not leak. Historical replay selects old valid/knowledge time but checks today's authority; offline private/shared replay cannot create a fresh release without current verification.

Tombstone immediately denies the parent and every influenced descendant: transcript/OCR, masks/tracks, embeddings, fusion/context, rejected outputs, personalized adaptation state, queued outputs and caches. An incomplete reverse-lineage index is not an eventually consistent security boundary: deny affected materialization and recompute only from current eligible sources. Track per-copy requested/ACK/verified-erased/unknown across exports/providers/backups. Inaccessible is not erased. Linkable hashes/receipts have no blanket retention exception; retain only under an explicit minimized audit purpose/access/retention policy.

A scene/view import is untrusted presentation intent and cannot resume embedded permits, jobs or schedules. It does not alone require rotating intact authority epochs. Restore/rollback of authority/evidence persistence or loss of continuity triggers CORE's separate restore protocol, new authority/restore epochs, review-only startup and trusted tombstone/revocation reconciliation. Existing jobs never silently restart from a saved view.

## F05 — Reasoning, intent, sensing and physical domains

ContextSnapshot is bounded eligible evidence, not a new source of authority. R14 WearerSession/SignalEvidence/IntentProposal remain separate from principal authentication and recipient selection. Button/gaze/EMG can propose a referent or finite action; confidence and confirmation cannot mint grants or arbitrary payloads. Diarized IDs and anonymous tracks never attach another person's health readings. Clinical interpretation is not authorized by a sensor reading.

A NextObservationRequest states question/ambiguity, frozen finite candidate, map/source/profile revisions, sensing footprint/participant scope, expected information gain or UNKNOWN, cost/time/bytes/energy, parent reservation, deadline and attempt. Use R07's existing record; no new acquisition service. Sensing and carrier motion require different domain authorities. R09 SkillIntent retains exact embodiment/qualification, local stop/recovery and UNKNOWN physical exclusion. Lease expiration or stopped telemetry cannot prove empty space, cessation or safe continuation. Digital help remains independent of carrier success.

## F06 — Publication, stops and budgets

Initial output profile is CORE `ONE_RELEASE_ONE_DESTINATION_WHOLE_OUTPUT`: immutable whole-output digest, exact destination/session/route/audience and one admitted release/consume slot. Keep four canonical records: ReleaseAuthorizationReceipt, ConsumptionPermitReceipt, DeliveryObservation and PermitEligibilityDecision. Atomically charge all applicable deduplicated finite grants at admitted release; redeem its already charged valid slot once after fresh eligibility without recharging. Duplicate receipt is history, not another send. Unknown commit/lost consume reply means read-only reconciliation, no automatic resend, new key, remint or refund.

Hold complete output before admission; no early private chunk. CORE laboratory envelope: two synthetic people,32 sources,16 required grants,128 lineage edges,depth16,metadata64KiB and output256KiB; reject excess rather than truncate closure. Media references do not exempt delivered assets from the output cap. Short TTS follows MM-AV whole-asset limits and independently checked audible fidelity. Audio plus text/haptics or destination handoff cannot silently reuse one-use authority. Streaming, recurrence and multidestination require separately accepted counting/manifest/sequence/cancel/buffer/exposure profiles and budgets, UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE otherwise.

Cancellation axes are independent: capture admission/actual capture stop; compute stop requested/independently confirmed; playback requested/observed; durable schedule cancellation; physical recovery requested/observed. Authenticated local stop may request narration stop; recorded speech or a picture saying stop is data. A flush ACK or UI disappearance is not proof remote buffers stopped, human perception did not occur, compute ended or billing ceased. Lost ACK never means not received. R14 cue requested/accepted/device-observed remains different from human interpreted/perceived; those latter claims need separate qualified study observations.

Each bounded worker/task uses CORE I04/I05 immutable payload/attempt, worker/boot, nonce, lease fence, parent-backed reservation and deadline. Failed/partial/tuning/retry work consumes the same inclusive cap. Unknown admitted use stays reserved/unsettled; child allocations cannot invent budget. Per-user queues reserve capacity for interactive receipts and cancellation; no research route starves another user's basic output. Fairness thresholds are a future measured profile, not an invented universal latency claim. No authorization failure triggers a stronger or hosted fallback.

## F07 — Minimum refusals and useful degradation

Structured reasons include UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE, SOURCE_INELIGIBLE, ORIGINAL_UNAVAILABLE, LINEAGE_INCOMPLETE, OUTPUT_PROFILE_UNSUPPORTED, ORDER_UNKNOWN, CLOCK_MAPPING_UNKNOWN, FRAME_OR_CALIBRATION_CONFLICT, SCALE_UNKNOWN, CORRELATION_UNRESOLVED, TRACK_LOST, RECIPIENT_AMBIGUOUS, RESOURCE_DENIED and OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. Minimize restricted details in displayed errors. If the unsupported part is distance, answer eligible visible color. If audio is missing, answer supported image-only facts and mark acoustic questions unavailable. If no current private authority exists, do not reconstruct a supposedly harmless summary from its cached influence. Useful degradation never weakens the domain boundary.
## F08 — Observatory SceneEntityProjection and replay export

Proposed profile IDs, not deployed schemas: `ri01.proposed.mmfusion.evidence-atom.v1`, `ri01.proposed.mmfusion.alignment-decision.v1`, `ri01.proposed.mmfusion.fusion-set.v1`, `ri01.proposed.mmfusion.scene-projection.v1`. Each references the exact native CORE/R07/MV/AV semantics, and unsupported consumers reject. H00 owns future wire/schema adoption; R15 owns presentation, not evidence authority.

SceneEntityProjection binds projection/view ID and revision; exact source EvidenceAtom/FusionSet refs/digests; native payload and original locator; origin/claim class separately from support state; valid/knowledge-time selection; native clock/sample/PTS and mapped support interval/uncertainty/gaps; frame/map/CRS/altitude/scale/calibration and SpatialSemantics refs; uncertainty kind/value or UNKNOWN; complete derivation/dependence refs; render transform/style version and simplification/loss; source-attribution/rights references; current complete authority closure; exact destination/route/audience; output digest and expiry. Render-generated motion or thermal styling is a presentation derivative with SIMULATED/STYLIZED class, never native telemetry/temperature.

A renderer unable to represent an essential class, unknown region, time gap, calibration or uncertainty must omit the affected claim/geometry or show a clearly unavailable, source-linked table. It may not silently present it as observed/current/metric. A lossy export stripping required semantics fails UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE. A permitted geometry-plus-sidecar export requires the sidecar by exact digest in the manifest, reimport validation and current rights; missing sidecar cannot be treated as a legacy trusted map. Source IDs, counts and metadata may themselves be sensitive, so eligibility/minimization applies to the export, not only pixel payloads.

Scene import restores presentation intent. New private replay/display/export requires fresh CORE release/consume and source closure. Ordinary import does not rotate an intact authority database, but restoration/rollback of authority persistence invokes the distinct CORE restore protocol. No embedded job/grant/permit resumes. This draft seam answers actual Q-R15-MMF-01; it is not independent acceptance of R15-AUDIT or a code-integration claim.

### F04/F06 clarification from accepted R11 public variant

Internet loss and authoritative-state loss are different. A complete eligible local verifier can perform current checks without Internet; a cached remote permission or unverified shared dependency cannot stand in for it. If full required current authority is unavailable, deny new private/shared release/replay while independently eligible public offline material remains useful. Encrypted relay is not a permission exception. Keep local storage, relay storage/transfer, destination transport/application, device output, human acknowledgment/perception and professional response as separately attributed observations. No network ACK proves human help; lost ACK does not prove nondelivery. R11 G1–G5 and exact native/provider/region/profile qualification remain; no emergency contact is sent by this design.
