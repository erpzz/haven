Original bytes/measurements remain typed and eligible under retention, not collapsed into prose. A source URL or code sample is data, not an execution/fetch instruction.

## Replaceable route comparison and rights/cost basis

Deterministic metadata, exact retrieval, unit checks, interval arithmetic and typed conflict handling need no model. Task processors can expose better-defined outputs: selected OCR/detection/masks, ASR, acoustic labels and calibrated geometry. General VLM/omni escalation may help combine ambiguous semantics but must preserve exact evidence and be evaluated on that task. Early feature-level fusion is deferred until it beats the late typed baseline under matched inputs, rights, attempt and resource budgets; no general superiority is asserted.

Reuse accepted MM-VISION SHORTLIST: classical OpenCV geometry/change, detection/OCR/segmentation and M4 advisory VLM alternatives. Reuse MM-AV CANDIDATES: bounded decode/DSP/VAD, Whisper/faster-whisper ASR, optional diarization, acoustic classifier, local short TTS and later omni/hosted alternatives. These remain dated, uninstalled candidate profiles. Software license, exact weight license, training/evaluation data rights, codec/build terms and hosted provider policy are separate. Do not infer all Qwen sizes, all Piper voices or all model wrappers share terms. No new model route or cloud price is selected by fusion.

For each future route freeze artifact/runtime/preprocessor/quantization, eligible inputs, current rights, warm/cold peak memory, preprocessing/decode/inference/retrieval/output time, latency distribution, bytes/energy, failed/retried attempts, installation/maintenance and human correction. Source benchmark numbers are not host measurements. Cost per useful authorized answer has an independently judged success denominator; false disclosures or fabricated measurements cannot be offset by a cheaper average. Parent-backed reservations prevent one user's research from starving another's receipts; no hidden paid escalation.
