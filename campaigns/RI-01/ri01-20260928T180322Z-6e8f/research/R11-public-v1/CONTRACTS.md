# Proposed H11 domain contracts

Namespace `ri01.proposed.h11.v1`; documentary extensions through CORE I01–I07, no new top-level service, deployed schema or accepted migration. All references are exact ID/revision/digest tuples; nullable unknowns carry a reason. Reject unsupported versions, nonfinite values, missing closure and ambiguous units. P0 finite-use policy remains `ONE_RELEASE_ONE_DESTINATION_WHOLE_OUTPUT`; H11 cannot silently expand it to multiple destinations, streaming or disconnected relay.

## CR-R11-01 IncidentDraft and LocationEvidence

`IncidentDraft{schema_version, incident_id, revision, exercise_only, principal_ref, subject_refs[], created_time, source_statement_refs[], observation_refs[], optional_interpretation_refs[], location_evidence_refs[], omitted_fields[{reason}], conflicts[], destination_proposal, authority_vector_ref, payload_digest, canonicalization_version, expires_at, retention_policy_ref}`.

Each statement is USER_REPORTED, OBSERVED, HISTORICAL, INFERRED or UNKNOWN with exact source and time. First profile rejects medical interpretation and external links/instructions supplied by untrusted evidence. Drafting never creates sending authority. User edits create a new revision and exact bytes; silent mutable drafts cannot reuse a release claim. No names, phone numbers, health history or exact location are default public fields.

`LocationEvidence{source_ref, source_class, capture_interval, source_clock_id, boot_epoch, utc_mapping_ref|null, time_uncertainty, received_at, processed_at, native_value, native_units, native_accuracy_semantics, position_wgs84|null, horizontal_accuracy|null, vertical_value|null, vertical_unit, vertical_datum:AMSL|ELLIPSOID|RELATIVE_HOME|UNKNOWN, geoid_or_home_ref|null, local_frame_ref|null, map_revision|null, transform_ref|null, covariance_ref|null, freshness_profile_ref, quality, conflicts[]}`.

Preserve the current accepted R07 spatial profile; this object does not choose a new transform/covariance convention. No [0,0] placeholder. Negative/invalid native accuracy is invalid, not zero error; a reported radius is not automatically a calibrated probability. Relative-home and local frames need exact origin/boot/configuration. Unknown mapping may support a qualified source-local description but blocks a current global/metric action claim. Last-known position is labeled with age/uncertainty, not interpolated through a gap as current. Human-entered landmark remains a separate source, never silently overwrites GNSS history. Floor/entrance association requires its own evidence.

## CR-R11-02 PathwayStatus and public portal

`PathwayStatus{pathway_id, kind:NATIVE_PHONE|LOCAL_PORTAL|SHORT_RELAY|INTERNET_BACKHAUL|SATELLITE_MESSENGER, exact_capability_tuple_ref, observation_time, valid_until, local_access_state, upstream_state, endpoint_reachability[{endpoint_class, test_ref, result, checked_at, age, error}], provider_service_state, power_state, route_profile_ref, metadata_classification, evidence_refs[]}`.

Each state supports UNKNOWN/UNAVAILABLE; online at one layer does not promote another. No connectivity check to a real endpoint is authorized by this schema. Endpoint reachability is service-specific, bounded and time-qualified. SSID, icon, DHCP lease, satellite lock or a mesh ACK does not establish Internet or emergency-service availability.

`PublicInfoBundle{bundle_id, revision, digest, publisher_ref, rights_ref, review_ref, language, accessibility_variant, issued_at, verified_at, expires_at, public_safe_field_allowlist, dependency_refs[], stale_presentation, correction_ref|null}`. Public access has no private account prerequisite. Keep guest resources separate from controls/private data/admin/sensors in routing as well as authorization. Never serve an internal status object by merely hiding its screen controls. A stale or unverifiable notice remains visibly qualified or withdrawn according to its reviewed profile. No cached source loses its correction/deletion dependencies because it is a PDF or printed export.

## CR-R11-03 immutable transport proposal

`TransportEnvelopeProposal{schema_version, profile_id, message_id, source_payload_digest, canonicalization_version, envelope_digest, encryption_profile_ref, recipient_binding_ref, destination_ref, route_profile_revision, allowed_relay_classes[], provider_data_policy_refs[], metadata_dependency_refs[], full_authority_vector_ref, source_closure_digest, release_receipt_ref, created_at, not_before, expires_at, maximum_age, source_clock_boot_ref, clock_uncertainty, copy_limit, hop_limit, byte_limit, attempt_limit, quota_reservation_ref, retention_policy_ref, cancel_scope_epochs[], replay_policy:NO_AUTOMATIC_REPLAY}`.

The fields do not create a transport capability. This proposed profile needs CORE/H04/H11 review and explicit operation rights for encryption, provider egress, relay storage/forwarding and destination consumption, including metadata. Recipient key identity and epoch must be verified through an approved enrollment/broker; key presence and radio-node ID alone do not authenticate a human. Do not implement cryptography in the proposed first packages. A digital envelope is not authority for moving its carrier.

**Current boundary:** P0 can represent one authorized whole output to one exact destination with fresh consumption. Disconnected private admission/forwarding is **DEFERRED**, including a relay that cannot determine current dependencies. The synthetic transport experiment manipulates fictitious envelope metadata and arbitrary bytes, never claims to exercise real protected forwarding. Previously admitted encrypted payloads are a research topic, not grandfathered offline permission. Internet absence alone is not a blocker when every required current dependency is locally resolvable under a reviewed profile; a stale cache is not current authority.

Admission atomically checks full CORE I01 equality, typed operation/purpose, expiry/time uncertainty, finite grant claims and resource budget and commits immutable receipts/outbox. No network or radio inside the authority transaction. Unknown commit means no send and read-only reconciliation. New recipient, changed payload, correction, route or audience needs new eligible admission. A second release cannot reuse an exhausted one-use grant. Existing charged claims may redeem their one slot after all fresh checks without requiring unused parent quota; consume does not charge again. Duplicates return historical records, not new usable permits, refunds or replay rights.

Semantic send replay after an unknown external outcome differs from lower-layer retransmission of identical bytes. A future transport profile must explicitly authorize and bound any retransmission/copy behavior, with exact envelope identity, per-hop observations, byte/attempt/expiry limits and destination deduplication. This proposal grants no such real transport behavior by implication. A duplicate bundle never earns a second human/application action or another consume permit.

## CR-R11-04 independent observations and current eligibility

Keep CORE `ReleaseAuthorizationReceipt`, `ConsumptionPermitReceipt`, `DeliveryObservation` and `PermitEligibilityDecision` unchanged. Attach domain `TransportObservation{observation_id, message_id, envelope_digest, destination_ref, release_sequence, consume_sequence|null, full_vector_ref, protocol, protocol_version, native_report_type, reporter_ref, authentication_evidence_ref|null, observed_time, received_time, clock_uncertainty, layer, outcome, evidence_ref, supersedes_observation_ref|null}`.

Layers are LOCAL_DURABLE_STORE, RELAY_TRANSFER, RELAY_STORE, DESTINATION_TRANSPORT, DESTINATION_APPLICATION, OUTPUT_DEVICE, HUMAN_ACKNOWLEDGMENT, PROFESSIONAL_RESPONSE. Outcomes are layer-specific and never cast into a higher layer. Native protocol observations may be UNAUTHENTICATED or UNKNOWN. An application ACK without content identity cannot acknowledge a different draft. A person may acknowledge receipt without understanding, agreement, dispatch or treatment. A claimed responder message remains an attributed message until the route and reporter are qualified; no synthetic receipt creates a real professional response.

UI examples: “Saved on this device”; “Relay reported storing the encrypted envelope”; “Destination application reported receipt; human receipt unknown”; “Sent, acknowledgment unavailable”; “Future display denied after withdrawal; earlier transfer cannot be recalled.” Never infer NOT_SENT from silence. BP status is preserved under `native_report_type`; BP application-agent delivery does not imply payload processing.

Revoke/correct/cancel is serialized with local release/consume at the accepted CORE ordering points, not with an imaginary global network transaction. Stop unsent future work when positively known; append in-flight uncertainty. Preserve historical delivery if later revoked. Fresh destination epoch/vector admission immediately before display is necessary but leaves a residual race to physical presentation. Do not promise recall or erase it by rewriting history. Reconnect revalidates before any new consume/replay. Unknown remote outcome is reconciled without automatic retransmission; an explicit replay is a new operation and may be ineligible.

## CR-R11-05 standing policy, cancellation and independent help

`StandingScenarioPolicy{policy_id, revision, authorizing_principal_ref, subject_scope, scenario_id, evidence_profile_ref, qualifying_evidence_window, max_evidence_age, valid_from, valid_until, one_use_claim_profile, ack_policy_ref, allowed_disclosures[], exact_destination_refs[], permitted_digital_actions[], separately_permitted_physical_actions[], safety_response_ref, cancel_scope_refs[], provider_region_qualification_ref, independent_help_deadline_profile_ref, authority_dependencies[]}`.

No production trigger thresholds are selected. Preserve S2/T33–35 as later synthetic scenarios; lack of a response is not fresh consent or medical evidence. Two-person grants do not merge. Consent to monitoring is not consent to disclosure, sending, sensing, movement or contacting an emergency service. Token scopes stay in the CORE broker; private tokens do not enter source packets/models. A safety stop may be locally accessible without granting read/disclosure/admin rights; its exact physical response remains domain-qualified. “Cancel send,” “cancel future repeats,” “withdraw capture,” “stop audio” and “stop carrier safely” remain separate actions with separate receipts.

The original standing test policy remains disabled and unavailable in M0–M6; S2 follows accepted M5 and is independent of S1. R11's inert policy fixtures neither modify that milestone boundary nor dispatch a simulated or real carrier.

`IndependentHelpProposal` uses CORE I07 with a dedicated sponsor/reservation, explicit synthetic/native mode, exact destination and bounded deadline. It cannot wait on optional inference, radio discovery, map completion or aircraft launch. Independent resource priority is a design constraint to test, not a measured guarantee. A native user-initiated call remains outside Haven's claimed delivery state unless an approved adapter provides qualified observations.

## CR-R11-06 readiness and outcome

`EquipmentReadiness{asset_id, exact_model_config, owner_ref, inspection_ref, inspection_time, inspection_expiry, battery_measurement_ref|null, inventory_revision, item_expiry, usable_state, qualification_refs[], transport_constraints, access_constraints, public_status_allowlist}`; missing inspection/qualification is UNKNOWN or UNAVAILABLE, never “ready” based solely on ownership.

`SupportOutcome{proposal_ref, carrier_domain_ref, destination_geometry_ref, dispatch_observations[], placement_observations[], human_access_observations[], output_observations[], use_observations[], uncertainty, professional_attribution_ref|null}`. Kit placement, accessible retrieval, understandable instructions, actual use and clinical outcome are independent. Ground speaker/light consumes its own output/safety/resource admission. A payload request cannot authorize flight or alter R10 recovery behavior. R09 logistics closure is pending; these are proposals, not peer consensus.

## CR-R11-07 deletion, restore and retention

CORE I02 tombstones immediately invalidate future eligibility across draft, source, model context, rejected output, queue, relay, destination cache, index, map, exports and derivatives. Track each copy's deletion requested/acknowledged/verified/unverifiable state. Backups restore into new authority/restore epochs, review-only, with revocations reconciled; no outbox automatically resumes. Deleting an encryption key does not prove all plaintext/copies are gone. Offline peers may remain unreachable.

Minimized noncontent audit records require a separately authorized retention policy, expiry and access scope; a digest, address, message graph or receipt can remain personal/linkable. No universal retention exception is created here. Public-safety providers' retention rules are not selected by Haven and must be evaluated before egress. Correction appends a new attributable record; it cannot overwrite prior human receipt or force an external professional to forget an already received message.
