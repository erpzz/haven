## Power and graceful degradation

The Starlink documents differ: a product page describes 25–40 W average, support says 20–40 W; both are manufacturer descriptions, not measured loads here. Power-adapter guidance around 65 W differs between support pages; exact approved supply/cable/firmware qualification must settle it. Do not infer that average consumption establishes sufficient startup/peak power. No wiring or battery construction is proposed.

For future planning, runtime estimate = usable energy in Wh divided by **total measured** terminal/router/phone/output average W, with conversion loss, aging, cold and reserve explicitly bounded. Rated charger W is capability, not stored energy. A reserve for basic communications/local status must not be silently spent on inference or payload light. Brownout, clock reset, damaged persistence and reboot should leave private queues fenced and public status honest; restored jobs cannot resume automatically.

The vendor AL1/AS1 laboratory values are useful comparative inputs, not a human-use qualification: AL1 max 32 W; AS1 max 15 W. DJI's accessory flight-time table reports a combined enabled condition of 35 W and 32.1 minutes flight versus 49 unloaded. The table's condition differs from summing individual maxima; it must not be converted into a conservative peak budget. Actual payload endurance/intelligibility/glare remains unmeasured. See S17/S18.

## Affordable staged choice

1. Review inert public help text and a two-person synthetic incident draft/receipt display. No new hardware, models or subscriptions.
2. Compare synthetic direct, stationary relay and intermittent-contact traces. Choose extra network complexity only for a defined useful improvement.
3. Under new authorization, verify one exact existing phone and isolated ground setup; qualify local-only portal, reserve power, selected private output and harmless ground light/kit usability.
4. Only after measured deficits justify it, compare a provisioned backhaul or messaging service and then optional carrier support. Preserve native/manual alternatives throughout.
