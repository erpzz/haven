## Negative evidence and scope discipline

The full rescue article was not fetched beyond the official abstract, so mechanism, adverse-event details and broad clinical efficacy remain outside this review. Producer specifications and an institutional case are different evidence classes. No source establishes an owned device, current local carrier service, reception at a specific place, battery runtime, intelligibility, human response, lawful region operation or a third-party native SOS entitlement.

Software rights, native SDK entitlements, hardware/regulatory compatibility, provider contract, participant/source-data authority and medical/professional scope require separate closure. No public availability of source code waives those boundaries. No source media was downloaded for a model study; all R11 experiment inputs are proposed synthetic or later separately licensed assets.
