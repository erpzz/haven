## E11-B — useful transport replay comparison

C17–C32: four development families, then twelve protected families C21–C32 with **four positive and eight protected negative**. At most two attempts per family, total **32 attempts inclusive of retries**. Sixteen unique contact traces; each attempt compares the same trace under direct, stationary-relay and moving-contact abstractions without inflating n to 48. Fixed modes run within the one attempt budget; no extra tuning after protected disclosure. No radio, movement, ION build, encryption or actual provider.

Use synthetic immutable envelope metadata with no private contents or real addresses; all route operations are model events. Compare messages arriving at the declared application deadline, useful payload bytes, energy proxy units, hops, duplicates, expiries, queue maximum, authority denials and receipt inflation. Proxy energy is not Wh and cannot select a battery. Ambition: discover when an additional relay actually helps and when direct/stationary is cheaper. No learned routing required. A moving contact is a trace, not motion permission.

Acceptance candidate: four protected useful results satisfied, eight protected families conservatively handled, zero layer inflation/unauthorized consumption; complete attempt ledger. Maximum four implementation hours, 32 attempts, ten seconds/attempt, 256 MiB if measurable, 10 MiB fixtures/output, zero network/models/USD. This adapts R12 O08 conceptually but does not claim its E08 protocol or original EX24 was run or equivalent. Private offline transport admission remains deferred even if replay appears successful.

## E11-C — later native and ground study

Separately authorized after E11-A/B independent review and exact hardware/native/region/rights closure. Stage one: one owned phone tuple and an isolated ground-only portal fixture, no emergency contacts. Demonstrate manual native-interface discovery without placing a call; local public help with upstream absent; private access denial; controlled restart; explicit output destination; measured reserve power. Stage two: ordinary ground light, phone/audio baseline and a dummy kit. Compare visibility/intelligibility/access rather than only lux, dB or coordinate arrival. No strobe, incident area, hazardous heat/brightness/noise, autonomous movement, treatment or human health collection.

Ambitious exercise: two separately consenting adults, twelve pre-scripted benign trials (two people × three conditions × two repetitions). Conditions: manual phone/public card; ground local portal/private draft; ground relay simulation with supervised equipment logistics. This is **n=2 participants**, not n=12 independent people; repetitions are dependent. Separate randomization/fatigue/accessibility and recording consent. Qualified human observers score critical message transcription, uncertainty comprehension, kit accessibility, time-to-useful-information and route/cancel truth; participants never encounter a fabricated real emergency. Freeze safe output levels, routes, accessibility alternatives and stop criteria before enrollment. Aircraft, live emergency-service integration and automatic standing-policy dispatch remain excluded; a later professional exercise is a separate decision.

Proposed ceiling: two setup hours plus one supervised session of at most 60 minutes; at most 12 trials / 24 output attempts inclusive of failed/retried outputs, zero paid API/model use, zero purchases under this proposal. Consent withdrawal, discomfort, unexpected bystander, data leak, uncertain electrical/output safety, uncontrolled network exposure or budget/containment failure stops immediately. No population reliability, clinical or rescue efficacy conclusion from this small study. Physical native outcomes cannot be accepted from metadata-only mocks.
