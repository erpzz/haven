# R14 proposed experiments and existing-domain interfaces

All eight cards: **NOT_EXECUTED; AUTHORIZATION_REQUIRED**. They are protocol candidates for independent H06 review, not studies started by publication. Proposed numerical success margins are decision criteria, not predicted effects or safety thresholds. Their precision does not constitute power analysis. Human-interface samples below are formative usability samples, never population efficacy or clinical evidence. No clinical, invasive, electrical stimulation, hazardous navigation or powered body-worn experiment is authorized or specified here.

## Common experiment accounting and evidence rules

Freeze the exact task, artifact rights, apparatus/host versions, oracle, development/held-out assignment, case IDs, candidate routes, metric definitions, exclusions and analysis before execution. Store all planned slots and outcomes, including failed, partial, cancelled and abstained attempts. Retries, tuning runs and replacements consume the same cap; missing required held-out coverage means incomplete/inconclusive, not a smaller denominator. Correlated variants and two route results are not independent participants. A study can stop early for a failure without claiming success. No card borrows the MM-AV 48-attempt budget or pools results with it.

For software cards, positive means a useful correct outcome on an answerable case; a refusal is not a positive success. Negative means correct uncertainty, rejection or containment according to a preregistered oracle. Wrong-user or unauthorized release is zero-tolerance for promotion. Keep signal quality, user task utility and authority integrity as separate endpoints. Freeze held-out family lists under an independent evaluator; authors may not tune on them. Record full cost: setup, review, calibration, compute, energy, storage, parts, maintenance and failed attempts.

For any later human card, a separately approved voluntary study must establish eligibility, informed consent, data rights/retention, compensation if any, accessible alternatives, withdrawal and immediate exit without explanation. No private physiological or audio recording is collected merely to assess a UI. An independent observer handles discomfort and stop requests. No roads, stairs, balance challenges, emergency response, sharp/hot objects, load-bearing tasks or medical outcomes. Clinical populations require their own review, not extrapolation from the formative sample. Stop on discomfort, fatigue, skin issues, confusing stop controls, privacy leakage or any unsafe apparatus state. Do not infer absence of harm from a small study.

Freeze the actual participant count before recruitment. The example E04–E06 decision rules assume four participants; fewer completions leave missing cases and a partial/inconclusive result, not a lower success threshold. Withdrawal is never overridden to finish a denominator. Record throughput, completion-time distributions, setup/calibration duration, missed commands, false activations per eligible idle hour, cue latency where physically observed, energy where measurable, effort/comfort and all adverse observations. Each card selects its primary task endpoint; the remaining measurements are descriptive, not additional unregistered success tests. Retention, cross-day refitting and cross-person generalization remain separate unproven claims unless their own protected conditions and budgets are later authorized.

### R14-PROP-E01 — Current memory versus ordinary notebook (software/replay)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** source-linked current decisions and transparent reminder states reduce wrong recall while preserving useful answers. **Baseline:** deterministic keyword/ID retrieval with a plain notebook/table; candidate is a scripted evidence-state presentation, no model. **Apparatus/data:** existing host, synthetic two-user notes, corrections, cancellation and delayed-delivery records; hashes and synthetic authorship ledger. No private files or calendar integration. **Ground truth:** independent case author specifies current decision, eligible audience and receipt state before evaluation.

**Design:** 24 independent families, 12 development and 12 held-out; each split six answerable and six protected privacy/staleness/ambiguity cases. Two routes, one run per family/route = **48 attempts inclusive**, zero planned retries. UI script and oracle freeze after development; no learned training. **Nuisances:** revoked uncited source, old cache, ambiguous recipient, cancelled reminder, duplicate ACK, offline return. **Analysis:** per-route positive correct/current-source count and negative containment count; target >=5/6 held-out positive and 6/6 negative, zero escapes. Also report source-inspection steps and latency; no claim of human memory benefit without a later study. **Budget:** four operator hours, two host hours, at most 1 GB output, $0 incremental spend; energy measured if instrumentation exists, otherwise UNKNOWN. **Stop/evidence:** any escape ends promotion; ledger, fixture hashes, all outputs and independent rubric retained. **Next gate:** H01/H03/H04 adoption review; actual inference remains CORE-gated. Links EX01/EX02/EX05/EX32 and their unchanged owners.

### R14-PROP-E02 — Intent is not authority (software/replay)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** a finite gesture classifier proposal can support useful selection without treating neural/muscle/voice output as authorization. **Baseline:** explicit synthetic button events; candidate: synthetic scored gesture events plus abstention and confirmation. **Apparatus/data:** inert state-machine fixtures, no biosignals or device. **Ground truth:** independent labelled intended action/session/principal/destination; labels represent synthetic intent, not decoded real intent.

**Design:** 20 families, ten development and ten held-out, each split five valid confirmations/five reject cases; two routes = **40 attempts inclusive**, no planned repeat. No training; synthetic event scores are not classifier accuracy evidence. **Nuisances:** idle motion, two wearers, stale session, drift flag, duplicate event, changed destination, injected instruction, lost confirmation ACK. **Analysis:** 5/5 held-out positives produce exactly the permitted draft; 5/5 negatives abstain/reject; zero sealed job or release minted from classification alone. Record clarification burden and unused valid intent, not only rejection count. **Budget:** four operator/two host hours, 1 GB, $0. **Stop/evidence:** stop on any authority bypass; retain state traces, source closure, oracle and rejected transitions. **Next gate:** rights/host dossier before E07; no physical actuator path. Links REQ-PRIV-06/08, REQ-DAILY-09, EX02/EX06.

### R14-PROP-E03 — Selected image, speech and stale spatial evidence (software/replay)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** explicit source/time/uncertainty presentation helps resolve a useful referent without inventing geometry or between-frame events. **Baseline:** selected image plus typed region reference and separate evidence list; candidate: synchronized event/region table. **Apparatus/data:** 18 synthetic scene families with two selected frames, typed speech reference and declared gaps; no actual audio/video inference. **Ground truth:** independently authored region identity, timing intervals, calibration applicability and answerable/unknown questions.

**Design:** six development and 12 held-out families, evenly positive/negative within each split; two routes = **36 attempts inclusive**, no repeats. Freeze transforms and uncertainty rules; no training. **Nuisances:** ambiguous “that,” overlapping clock uncertainty, dropped frame, corrected map, unknown scale, false acoustic event, correlated crop/detection, old user grant. **Analysis:** candidate must correctly resolve >=5/6 held-out positives and all six negative cases; no asserted ordering with overlapping uncertainty, no visual claim between samples, no metric claim without calibration. Report route-specific usefulness, inspection steps and evidence-state mistakes; this tests protocol representation, not perception. **Budget:** five operator/two host hours, 2 GB, $0. **Stop/evidence:** stop promotion on identity/authority/time fabrication; retain originals/derived manifests and independent expected facts. **Next gate:** MM-VISION/MM-AV/H08 protocol review before actual media/model evaluation. Links EX09/EX17/EX31; EX17's H10 ownership remains unchanged.

### R14-PROP-E04 — Three tactile states versus a screen badge (low-risk interface)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** a small cue vocabulary lowers screen checking without raising missed-task or confusion rates. **Baseline:** visible badge alone; candidate: same badge plus three cues. **Apparatus/data:** only an already qualified commercial haptic endpoint or off-body bench indicator, exact R04 tuple and physical-output observability; synthetic notifications, no health data. If endpoint support is unknown, the study does not start. **Ground truth:** randomized commanded cue identity and separately observed output; receipt is not assumed perception.

**Design:** up to four independently consenting adults under a separately reviewed protocol. Per person two practice trials/route plus eight scored trials/route (four cue/answerable and four withheld/uncertain conditions): **20 attempts/person, 80 total inclusive**, no planned repeats. Practice calibration includes comfortable user-selected ordinary device settings, not an invented physiological threshold. Route order counterbalanced; new cue instances held out from practice. **Nuisances:** delay, missing cue, competing benign task, quiet setting, duplicate cue; no emergency cues. **Analysis:** within-person task completion, cue confusion, screen checks, missed primary-task events and reported effort; candidate target >=20% fewer screen checks with no extra task errors for at least three of four people, no privacy escape. Report every person and uncertainty; n=4 cannot establish efficacy. **Budget:** 30 minutes/person maximum including breaks, six staff hours total, 500 MB aggregate, $0 purchase; any incentive or hardware requires a revised approved total. **Stop/evidence:** participant stops/withdrawals preserved; logs contain no physiology. **Next gate:** H07/H01/H06 review, with observed output versus perceived cue kept distinct. Links EX07/EX31 and REQ-WEAR-08.

### R14-PROP-E05 — Gaze proposal plus button confirmation (low-risk interface)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** supported OS gaze selection reduces reaching while retaining accurate referents. **Baseline:** touch/mouse selection; candidate: OS gaze/dwell proposal followed by explicit button confirmation. **Apparatus/data:** exact already-owned supported phone/host, benign images and fixed regions; no gaze export assumed, no camera stream retained. OS permission/model/build and accessible fallback independently checked first. **Ground truth:** task card names intended region; independent observer records selected/confirmed region and elapsed task time.

**Design:** up to four separately consenting adults; two calibration/practice and eight scored trials per route/person = **80 total attempts inclusive**. Four positive and four ambiguous/changed-region scored tasks per route; independent held-out image instances, counterbalanced order. No model training. **Nuisances:** ordinary comfortable repositioning, benign lighting variation, stale selected image, ambiguous task; no forced fatigue. Recalibration time counts against completion time. **Analysis:** at least three of four participants achieve >=20% fewer manual pointing actions without worse wrong-confirmation rate or higher self-reported burden; report time and all failures even if threshold met. No eye-tracking accuracy claim without raw ground truth. **Budget:** 30 minutes/person, six staff hours, 500 MB, $0 purchase. **Stop/evidence:** discomfort or unresolvable calibration ends that person's session; no substitute participant to erase failure. **Next gate:** H01/H07 accessibility review; raw-gaze API remains a separate unsupported proposal. Links EX08/EX31, REQ-WEAR-05/06/08.

### R14-PROP-E06 — Passive extra-effector form versus an ordinary clamp (low-risk bench/interface)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** an unpowered extra holding surface helps a benign tabletop task after setup and removal costs. **Baseline:** no aid and ordinary stationary clamp; candidate: unpowered mock form, **PHYSICAL_ACTUATION_UNSUPPORTED**. **Apparatus/data:** independently reviewed soft/lightweight mock or existing passive fixture and harmless lightweight item, no fabrication now, no load-bearing wearable, sharp/hot object or powered motion. Exact fit/material/retention/removal review is a precondition. **Ground truth:** fixed task completion/placement rubric and independent observer; no force/biomechanical claim.

**Design:** up to four separately consenting adults, three routes; one practice + four scored tasks/route/person = **60 attempts inclusive**. Two straightforward and two awkward-layout cases/route; layout held out from practice, order counterbalanced. No training beyond familiarization. **Nuisances:** don/doff, comfortable posture change, dropped harmless item, interrupted task and explicit removal. **Analysis:** completion and placement quality, setup/removal time, interference and perceived effort. Candidate must beat the better ordinary baseline by >=15% in median total task time without worse completion or removal issues for at least three people; descriptive only. A clamp winning is useful evidence. **Budget:** 30 minutes/person, eight staff hours, $150 complete provisional parts ceiling only after a separately approved quote, 500 MB. **Stop/evidence:** any snag, discomfort, unstable fixture or difficult removal stops; removal REQUESTED/ACK/OBSERVED/UNKNOWN recorded. **Next gate:** H09/H10/H07 independent review; no automatic powered escalation. Related inherited EX13 addresses later measured fixture repeatability; EX16 addresses any later sensor-enclosure effect. These retain H09 ownership and do not qualify body contact or powered behavior.

### R14-PROP-E07 — Does sEMG add signal beyond priors? (ambitious staged, offline first)

**Hypothesis/outcome:** exact licensed sEMG data improve a small deliberate-input task on held-out people and sessions beyond ordinary feature and nuisance controls. **Baseline:** constant/majority label, lightweight fixed feature rule, and matched shuffled-signal control; candidate: one pinned released neuromotor checkpoint. Generative decoding, if ever selected, must additionally include LM-only and shuffled-signal/label controls with identical prompt/context; that is a new protocol variant, not free added runs. **Apparatus/data:** S02 exact release/commit, sample/channel/preprocessing identity and S03/checkpoint rights; host resource/lifecycle qualification before any download or run. No new human signals, acquisition board or cloud upload.

**Stages/design:** Stage A rights/artifact/host dossier; Stage B independent immutable split; Stage C bounded offline study only on approval. Use 24 independent person/session families, 12 development and 12 held-out, each six deliberate positive and six idle/nuisance/uncertain families. Four routes ×24 = **96 attempts inclusive**, zero planned retries; one checkpoint/one fixed feature rule only; no fitting or model training in this default variant. If the release cannot supply eligible idle/nuisance families or distinct required sessions, STOP/REDESIGN before freeze; do not fabricate them from labelled gesture windows. No training on held-out people or future sessions. Training/fit calls, if any, require explicit slots within96 and reduce available coverage; absent complete coverage is inconclusive.

**Calibration/nuisances:** exact channel order/rate, preprocessing, participant and session separation; placement, posture and motion strata only where metadata supports them. **Analysis:** candidate must exceed strongest admissible baseline on at least five of six positive held-out families with all six protected negatives appropriately abstained, no authority output and no advantage on shuffled controls. Report family-level effect sizes, per-person outcomes, confusion, false activations per eligible idle duration, abstention/coverage and actual cold/warm resources; no population superiority claim from24 families. Zero observed false activations is not a zero population rate. **Budget:** eight operator hours, four host hours, 20 GB storage and $0 spend/download fee; exact resource ceiling must fit host before approval. **Stop/evidence:** stop on rights ambiguity, leakage, unavailable idle labels, host starvation or budget; save all attempts and model/data/license hashes. **Next gate:** H12/H07/H02/H06; separate consented acquisition study only after a complete new protocol, not authorized by offline success.

## Proposed interface extensions — no new service or authority shortcut

Namespace `ri01.proposed.r14.v1`; documentary records only. Every ref carries immutable type/id/revision/hash/canonicalization identity and the existing CORE authority/dependency closure. No shared schema is edited and no flag activates an executor. The existing owners decide whether these extend their interfaces; R14 cannot independently adopt them.

Common envelope for every record: producer/consumer role, data subject distinct from authenticated principal, exact current audience/destination, purpose/operation, source and consent revisions, creation/capture/arrival clocks, expiry, quality/uncertainty, cancel/lease/budget/restore epochs and immutable payload hash. Units are explicit per quantity; unknown units, time mapping or calibration reject claims that require them. Proposed errors are WEARER_UNBOUND, INTENT_AMBIGUOUS, CALIBRATION_EXPIRED, SIGNAL_QUALITY_UNKNOWN, UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE, OUTPUT_ROUTE_CHANGED and PHYSICAL_ACTUATION_UNSUPPORTED, carried as R14-PROP-Ixx proposed semantics rather than new canonical IDs. Minimized error text uses its own eligible audience. Retry requires fresh eligibility inside the original cap; unknown external output is reconciled read-only.

Producer/consumer flow: H07's explicitly enrolled native adapter produces session/signal evidence for H08/H02 interpretation and H04 eligibility; the bounded H02 interpreter produces an intent proposal for H01's user confirmation; H01 produces an output request for H07's exact endpoint and H04 release/consume checks. H12 experiment designers produce adaptation records for independent H06 evaluation. H01 may submit a confirmed skill proposal to H10, whose separate qualified controller alone could execute a newly sealed domain job. H09 supplies fixture/material/maintenance evidence to H07/H10; neither an LLM nor the R14 researcher owns physical qualification. These are logical boundaries, not a service deployment plan.

All influencing ancestors count, including uncited context, cached features, personal calibration, labels and models where their use restrictions apply. Derivative removal, withdrawal and correction must propagate through current eligibility without rewriting history or silently restoring deleted access. CORE release, finite-use consumption, delivery observation and current eligibility remain four separate records. The first finite output profile is one release/one destination/whole output; live chunked speech, streaming haptics, bilateral telepresence or multiple recipients need separately explicit profiles. Lost ACK does not authorize replay/remint/refund. A wearer session does not establish a listener's private audience.

Revocation can prevent later eligible use but cannot recall information already heard, seen or delivered. Deletion outcomes for raw, derived, model, cache, device queue and backup copies remain separately requested/acknowledged/verified/unknown. Local processing and application scopes do not protect against a malicious host administrator; no such guarantee is made. A future human study must measure learning/retention and cross-day refitting separately before making longitudinal claims; the small E04–E06 formative studies do not establish those outcomes.

Keep source time base, exact timestamps/sample ranges/PTS, clock mapping version, uncertainty, actual selected-frame list and uncovered/dropped intervals. Captions, waveform features and selected frames are correlated with their source. Acoustic support does not reveal a visual event between frames; UNKNOWN_VISUAL_BETWEEN_SAMPLES remains. LSL clock correction is a candidate method, not timing qualification; acquisition delay and offline/online processing differ. An event ordering claim requires non-overlapping applicable timing uncertainty. No coordinate/thermal/depth/force claim comes from an uncalibrated language-model description.
