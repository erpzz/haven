## Plain-English partner explanation — 150 words

Haven could become easier to use without requiring unusual equipment. The first useful experiments would compare familiar buttons, reminders, captions and simple tools with new ways of choosing information or receiving a quiet cue. A small vibration might mean that information is missing, while looking at an object could help select it before you confirm. Muscle signals might eventually provide discreet shortcuts, but they would never identify you or authorize an action by themselves. We would first check whether these ideas actually save effort and avoid mistakes. Each person would keep separate private information and control when any recording starts or stops. Extra fingers, advanced textiles and neural interfaces remain research possibilities with additional costs and specialist requirements. Nothing has been tested on people or connected to hardware here. The next decision is whether to approve a small synthetic comparison, which could reveal useful improvements before buying anything at all.
