# H07 proposed extensions to accepted CORE

Status: design proposal, not an executed implementation or an independently accepted schema. Canonical upstream is CORE `integration/core-v1/CONTRACTS.md`, SHA `df19f37926b33851236aca230859adfef332a85120380ea04c24294237c2e6d0`. Add domain fields without dropping any I01 AuthorityVector dependency or bypassing I06. Bounded first profile: selected still / one destination / whole immutable output; no finite-use streaming or multicasting.

## W01 DeviceCapabilityRecord

Fields: schema/version, device logical ID, enrollment subject and principal separately, device-binding revision/epoch, exact manufacturer/model/SKU/hardware revision, OS/build, firmware, native app/SDK/package version and artifact digest, companion/paired topology, country/feature eligibility evidence, entitlements/distribution channel, capabilities keyed individually, last probe time, source documentation version, qualification receipt and expiry. Every capability has `UNKNOWN | DOCUMENTED | OBSERVED_UNQUALIFIED | QUALIFIED_FOR_SCOPE | UNAVAILABLE` plus supported operation, mode, transport, foreground/background states, limits and evidence.

Capabilities include selected-media import, still camera, microphone, encoded/raw stream, audio output, display, haptic, intentional input, health type, battery/thermal/don state and stop reporting. Absence of one never implies absence or presence of another. A camera-only product gets phone-display fallback; an unavailable preview feature is unavailable, not emulated by silently broadening capture. An OS/SDK/firmware change invalidates affected qualification. Local administrator control is not cryptographic device attestation; device state can be compromised.

## W02 HealthObservation and NativeAccessObservation

HealthObservation extends I02: immutable observation ID/revision; explicit subject-binding evidence and quality; source provider/app/device reference; vendor sample ID; type and estimator identity; raw value/unit plus normalized value/unit and conversion version; original start/end interval; receive/processing time; clock/boot mapping and uncertainty; provider quality/metadata/classification with algorithm version; manual/imported/device provenance; query scope/anchor epoch; correction/deletion links; full rights/retention/grant parents. A structured metadata digest is labeled as such, not a hash of unretained sensor bytes.

Separate `observed_at_source`, `received_at_bridge`, `received_at_haven` and `processed_at`. Clock uncertainty or an interval extending into an unexplained future forbids a current claim. Freshness output is `HISTORICAL | CURRENT_WITHIN_QUALIFIED_TASK_BOUND | STALE_FOR_TASK | UNKNOWN`, with the actual age/interval and task-bound policy revision. No universal clinical threshold is supplied. Provider confidence is not confidence of subject identity or medical truth. Summaries retain measurement-versus-interpretation distinction and cannot manufacture a value from an empty result.

NativeAccessObservation records API/version, requested types, app capability/entitlement state, authorization request outcome, query result/error/visibility window and time. For Apple HealthKit, `READ_VISIBILITY_UNKNOWN` is often the honest result: authorization completion and `authorizationStatus(for:)` do not reveal read consent. New restricted-window APIs require exact availability qualification. Empty queries may mean no samples, denied or restricted access, unavailable hardware, synchronization delay or query scope. Do not log private permission guesses as fact. Do not poll for physiology by interpreting denial patterns.

HavenOwnGrant remains an independent revocable permission. A user selecting Pause/Withdraw in Haven immediately changes its authoritative scope/epoch and denies new admission even if the OS still grants read access. An OS Settings read withdrawal may not be observable with equivalent precision; native queries enforce platform access, while already imported data remains subject to its separate Haven retention/deletion decision. No guarantee of immediate remotely observable OS withdrawal is made. Enrollment explains this limit and provides an in-app stop/delete path. A cached Boolean cannot substitute for current native API enforcement or the complete Haven vector.

Cursor ingestion proposal: bind subject/type/query predicate/store epoch; commit added/corrected/tombstoned observations and anchor together; idempotently deduplicate; reject cross-subject or wrong-store anchors. Tombstones invalidate summaries, embeddings, charts and exports at the authority boundary. An anchored deleted-object callback is useful evidence, not proof of all provider deletion or revocation events. Synchronization unknown is distinct from an empty authorized history.

## W03 CaptureSession

Fields: capture-session ID, principal/subject/device binding and epochs, explicit start intent/receipt, requested modality and operation, selected scope/area/participants, bounded duration/bytes/frames, hardware permission observation, capability tuple, source clock/boot, local indicator state, retention/egress destination/purpose, full I01 vector and stop policy. Initial slice selects one existing image; future direct capture has separate permission. Microphone, background collection, continuous streaming and cloud egress are independent admissions.

State separates requested start, native start report, current eligibility, acquisition stop request, stop report and qualified cessation observation. When permission/session/route/subject/cancel changes, fence future acquisition and release, request native stop, quarantine late frames and discard unauthorized payloads under retention policy. A successful stop API call alone is not proof that every hardware buffer stopped. Device loss, unobserved wearer swap and unreachable adapter can leave cessation UNKNOWN. Surface that state; do not claim covert continuation is impossible based on a mock.

Bystander and environment constraints are part of participant/area scope. Capture indicator and user-visible pause must be native-qualified; a product brochure is insufficient. A wearing sensor may detect removal but cannot recognize a new wearer. A new or uncertain wearer requires explicit re-binding; a camera face match cannot silently attach A's health data to B. The system does not initiate public surveillance or identify bystanders.

## W04 CueRequest and DestinationHandoff

CueRequest: explicit trigger and purpose, intended authenticated recipient, exact device/session/route, cue type and bounded duration, corresponding message/source references if any, validity interval, full authority vector, idempotency and cancel scope. `CUE_REQUESTED`, `PLATFORM_ACCEPTED`, `PLAYBACK_REPORTED`, `UNKNOWN` are not synonyms for “person understood.” A cue that signals sensitive activity is itself a disclosure. Prefer a generic optional cue with an accessible visible alternative. No cue means no conclusion about condition, intent or location.

DestinationHandoff binds old and new destination identities, exact requested content digest, new route/audience, current lineage and new admission. It is not a transfer of an old bearer permit. Under P0, a cross-device replay is a new release/use claim and may fail if a finite parent grant is exhausted. Explicit replay is not a silent quota refund/remint. Quota accounting revision remains separate from authorization revision. Stop the old route first where possible, record overlap uncertainty, and revalidate before new output; perfect atomic handoff across physical devices is not claimed.

## W05 Ordering and output failure semantics

Reuse CORE I06's four records unchanged: immutable ReleaseAuthorizationReceipt, immutable ConsumptionPermitReceipt, append-only DeliveryObservation and current PermitEligibilityDecision. Their identity binds output digest/canonicalization, whole-output/chunk identity, exact destination/route/audience, release/consume sequences and the full vector. For finite P0, only whole-output/single-destination is admitted. A fresh release charges once; its already charged valid claim can consume its one slot without charging again, while another new release is denied at exhausted quota.

The short authoritative transaction compares current sources/grants/policy/cancel/lease/session/device/destination/restore state and writes the use claim/outbox/receipt atomically. No hardware or network operation is inside that transaction. Unknown commit means no send pending read-only reconciliation. Before display/playback, online consume rechecks full eligibility and the destination checks its authenticated session/route/epoch and exact payload. No fresh private/shared release or replay while that verification is unavailable.

Revocation before release denies admission; after release it denies later consumption as applicable; after consumption it fences future work but can race already buffered display/audio. The already shown content cannot be recalled. Lost ACK stays DELIVERY_UNKNOWN/SENT_UNACKNOWLEDGED through revocation. A past DISPLAY_REPORTED remains historical after cancellation. NOT_SENT/SUPPRESSED require positive evidence of a prevented unsent operation, not lack of ACK. Watch transfer callbacks, haptics and vendor SDK return values provide only their stated evidence scope.

Route loss: stop private playback and mask pending private UI, retain independent unknown/success history, request safe local halt, and require fresh destination admission after reconnection. Never fall back to speaker, lock-screen plaintext or another household device. Cleartext already rendered or OS-controlled notification history may persist; native qualification must measure and document limits. Stop-talking, cancel-job, withdraw-data, delete-history and request physical recovery stay separate controls.

## W06 Retention, compromise and emergency boundaries

Raw image/audio/health, requests, context, rejected model output, caches, embeddings, notifications, exports, native queues, vendor processors and backups each need explicit purpose, access and expiry. A minimal receipt may still be sensitive through IDs/times/hashes. Deletion first tombstones and denies eligibility, then records per-copy requested/acknowledged/verified/unknown outcomes; withholding is not erasure. Restore starts review-only under new authority/restore epochs and reconciles current tombstones. No automatic stream, timer or token resumes.

Compromised source/authenticity or untrusted clock changes advance the relevant source epochs, invalidate dependent claims and block affected current-action assertions. A hardware-reported measurement is not immune to wrong-subject, altered-device or replay risk. Suspicious payload text, QR codes and spoken instructions remain data, not authority. Provider/account secrets stay behind scoped native/broker boundaries, never model context or public receipts.

Wearable data may support a separately reviewed emergency proposal, not diagnose or execute it. Standing emergency disclosure policy needs its own persons, purpose, scope, expiry and review; physical dispatch retains independent domain permits and qualified capability. Lack of a reading, a fall callback, a haptic or a silent response cannot grant motion/contact authority. R11 owns independent help-channel research; R14 owns later deliberate-signal/human-factors qualification.
