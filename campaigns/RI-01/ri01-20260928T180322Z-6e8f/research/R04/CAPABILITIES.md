## Health measurement semantics

| Requested quantity | Apple representation | What may truthfully be shown | Must not infer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Heart rate / resting heart rate | `heartRate`, `restingHeartRate` quantity samples | Recorded value, unit, interval, source and quality metadata if present; eligible session value only with sample age | Guaranteed live feed or current heart rate from a delayed history sync |
| HRV | `heartRateVariabilitySDNN` | SDNN interval statistic and units | Stress diagnosis; equivalence to Android RMSSD or another estimator |
| Respiratory rate | `respiratoryRate` | Recorded rate and actual interval | Continuous breath-by-breath waveform or real-time respiratory status |
| Sleep | `sleepAnalysis` category samples | Recorded sleep intervals/categories with source/version and later corrections | Current sleep or unconsciousness from no phone/Watch response |
| Wrist temperature | `appleSleepingWristTemperature` | Read-only overnight aggregate; HealthKit absolute wrist value versus Health app relative baseline are different representations | Core temperature, fever diagnosis or an on-demand thermometer; Health display delay implies no samples |
| Oxygen | `oxygenSaturation` | Available recorded percentage/fraction with explicit unit conversion and source | All models/countries supported or a medical-grade/current measurement |
| ECG | `HKElectrocardiogram` + `HKElectrocardiogramQuery` | A recorded ECG and source-provided classification/algorithm version where permitted | Continuous ECG, a Haven diagnosis or an unrestricted raw-sensor stream |
| Blood pressure / glucose | HealthKit includes corresponding data types | Only genuinely supplied observations from an identified supported source | Apple Watch physically measures either simply because the datatype exists |
| Fall | `numberOfTimesFallen` history versus separate Core Motion event | Original provider event/count and time, retained as unconfirmed context | Fall certainty, identity, incapacitation or authority to dispatch help |

Sources S05–S10,S14,S17. Measurement availability depends on model, OS, settings, age restrictions and country. Current Apple support describes a US-specific blood-oxygen path for some newer purchased devices that analyzes on iPhone; therefore neither “all US Watches disabled” nor “all third-party apps can read every US device” is justified. Exact HealthKit exposure must be qualified. No medical interpretation is proposed.
