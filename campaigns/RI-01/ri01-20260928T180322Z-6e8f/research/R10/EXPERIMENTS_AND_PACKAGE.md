## Smallest useful experiments

**R10-E0 / original EX18, R0/R01.** After the operator supplies already-public exact model/controller/app identifiers or a public manual, spend at most the original four hours resolving official control, telemetry, video, authentication, OS and offline support. Retain each search and contradictory source. Stop at four hours or exact supported/unsupported finding. UNKNOWN is a legitimate result and does not trigger device probing. Cost ceiling zero new spend; reviewer H06/H05. This task only established that input remains missing, not that the four-hour experiment was run.

**R10-E1 / EX19 documentation refinement.** Freeze one candidate's exact supported tuple and complete delivered BOM with current official sources; compare manual/simulator baseline and one alternative. Cap future research at three hours, no procurement. Fail selection if any required API, firmware compatibility, total ceiling, takeover pathway or critical component remains unpriced/unverified. An unavailable retailer listing stays unavailable. Documentation success authorizes no connection.

**R10-E2 / P0 below.** Use synthetic JSON plus an original synthetic image of benign objects; produce a source-linked local record and admission result. Vary boot/frame/time/configuration/grant fields; show unavailable rather than inventing position or permission. No SDK, socket, live database, model, account or aircraft is required. This tests software semantics only and gives the world inspector a useful honest aircraft evidence view.

**R10-E3 / later EX19 read-only qualification.** Separately authorized ground session on an identified supported aircraft, 90-minute cap, no automatic motion interface, no new network exposure. Exact operational procedure and battery/motor safety must be supplied by qualified operator before scheduling. Compare a prearranged non-personal visual change against camera capture/receive times, state freshness and disconnect/reconnect. Retain all gaps, latency percentiles and clock uncertainties; qualify only the exact observed function. If no camera-origin timestamp exists, preserve UNKNOWN or measured bounded relationship without relabeling receive time. Stop on unexpected arming/motion, wrong identity, privacy breach, thermal/battery concern or unknown authority. R10 supplies no step-by-step hardware procedure.

## More ambitious but bounded experiments

**R10-E4 / original EX20, S1/T32.** After accepted M5, use two explicitly isolated qualified simulated vehicles with distinct system/instance/boot IDs; disjoint routes, recovery areas and landing reservations. Drop one telemetry stream; its exclusion remains, the other does not inherit its commands or pad. Inject a stale scheduler epoch and collision of requested slots. Stop after the original S1 gate and retained timelines; zero real endpoints. This remains separately authorized from current single-vehicle M1–M5 work.

**R10-E5 / battery handoff extension.** Synthetic two-vehicle scheduler model, maximum12 scenarios in one90-minute analysis run after E4 design review. Vary replacement readiness, origin reset, battery uncertainty, original reserve, pad occupancy and lost acknowledgement. No output may claim uninterrupted observation while neither vehicle provides fresh qualified evidence. The original recovers on its own bounded policy when replacement is unavailable. Later actual battery/flight data must replace synthetic assumptions before physical use.

**R10-E6 / EX16 and EX22.** First compare a ground-mounted benign light/speaker against the same task without it: read a nonpersonal sign, notice a path marker, understand a short neutral message. Specify exact output device, electrical supply, audience, exposure/volume limits, ambient conditions and human consent before any test. Two-hour future session cap; no aircraft attachment. Record task accuracy/intelligibility and energy/thermal observations, including failures. A later separately reviewed payload study measures all-up mass/CG/power and flight effects; ground success is not airworthiness evidence.

**R10-E7 / EX21, S2/T33–T35.** After accepted M5, independent of S1, preserve the original synthetic standing policy and test-inbox destination. Qualifying synthetic event plus configured no-ack interval may yield one exact simulated mission authorization; policy expiry/revocation, prior acknowledgement, unrelated missed reminders or consumed launch limit deny. Mock help queues on its own deadline even if model stopped, aircraft unavailable or mission rejected. A local accepted receipt is not actual help dispatched. No medical inference or real emergency-provider contact.

**R10-E8 / EX24 communications and base readiness.** Offline simulated contacts only, at most12 cases: bridge loss with vendor link healthy, cloud/backhaul loss, video congestion, duplicated/delayed receipts, mains loss, pad occupancy and charging fault. Store-forward distinguishes enqueued, locally transmitted, remotely acknowledged and delivery unknown. Dock ready cannot be inferred from heartbeat or battery percentage alone. Physical modem/radio/site integration needs a new jurisdiction and device assignment.
