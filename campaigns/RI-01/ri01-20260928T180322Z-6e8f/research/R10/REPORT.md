### Batteries, handoff, payloads and bases

Initial operation is sequential with human battery replacement. Model usable energy under the exact payload, temperature, wind, battery health and required recovery reserve; advertised maximum endurance is not a mission budget. Observe battery identity and health, charger completion and inspection age. Begin handoff only if the replacement is presently ready, its ingress/egress and landing resources are reserved, and the original retains its own bounded recovery margin. A replacement that fails to launch or loses communications cannot force the original to loiter beyond reserve. Gaps in coverage are explicitly unavailable.

The payload review includes mass with bracket/wires, center of gravity, attachment retention, propulsion margin, peak/average electrical draw, voltage/current limits, brownout isolation, heat, aerodynamic effects, RF interference, sensor occlusion, vibration, weather ingress and landed inspection. A mount hole, printed bracket or vendor payload number does not qualify a custom assembly. Ground mounting is the affordable first light/speaker experiment. On-aircraft lights can alter vision exposure and glare; rotor noise and orientation affect speech intelligibility. A speaker's rated sound pressure does not establish understood instructions. Keep output volume/exposure and audience appropriate to the future approved test; do not attach private health content to public audio.

For a future RF/spatial payload, retain REV-R06's apparatus and subject-footprint limits: cropping a returned map does not contain acquisition coverage, and aircraft mobility changes calibration and covered subjects. A simple radio link is not coherent radar or through-wall semantic perception. Any reuse of R06's active-observation experiment keeps **60 seconds total with at most8 attempted observations**, not60 seconds per selection; slower decisions consume the remaining episode budget. Sensor stop request, acknowledgement and confirmed cessation remain separate. No RF payload, collection or flight is authorized here.

Dock 3 documentation provides a matched enterprise reference with one-aircraft accommodation, charging and supported Matrice payloads. It does not establish a universal dock, battery swapping, occupancy sensing adequacy, legal unattended launch or Haven integration. Retain dock recovery cases: occupied/obstructed pad, weather outside envelope, inaccurate landing, contact contamination, thermal/charging fault, stale inspection, mains loss, backhaul loss and person entering the area. Earliest base is a recorded manual pad/charger inventory; automated charging comes after measured landing and electrical qualification. [Dock specifications](https://enterprise.dji.com/dock-3/specs).

### Communications and jurisdiction

Keep four links distinct: aircraft command/control, telemetry, high-rate video, and off-site help/backhaul. A healthy vendor radio does not prove the phone bridge is healthy; a Wi-Fi SSID, LoRa link or mesh does not establish internet reachability or broadband video capacity. Local store-and-forward carries bounded signed/digested evidence and acknowledgement status, not assumed remote receipt. Drop stale video, retain declared gaps and prioritize qualified control/recovery traffic; reserve separately measured bandwidth and compute. Do not put safety control through an unqualified remote model call. DJI Cloud API uses standard cloud transports for supported equipment, but that is not an instruction to expose a broker or grant network access. [Cloud API](https://developer.dji.com/cloud-api/).

Registration/Remote ID applicability must be checked for the exact operation and equipment; a small consumer aircraft's marketing weight alone is insufficient.
