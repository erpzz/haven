## Decisions

### D1. Helpful personality with explicit control

Haven is calm, direct and warm: answer first, briefly explain uncertainty, cite sources, and offer one useful next step. “I found the corrected decision from Tuesday” serves the person better than policy narration. Preferences for detail, language and channel are editable per person; they cannot expand providers, tools, recipients or physical scope.

Do not simulate private thought between sessions, imply emotional dependence, use guilt/fear to retain attention, or manufacture familiarity from sensitive history. Repair a mistake with the corrected fact and affected outputs. A rejected recommendation does not trigger persuasion to defend the assistant. Voice, face and assistive settings do not establish identity, disability, emotion or authority.

Neither user enrolls the other. Shared equipment or a relationship does not imply shared private history. A shared conversation is an explicitly selected destination with its own audience; joining it does not expose either private workspace. Borrowed devices and changed output routes need trusted binding checks.

### D2. Proactivity and notifications

WAI supports easy interruption control [S03]; Apple advises restraint and privacy in notifications [S02]. Proposed numeric policy:

| Class | Default and user control | Expiry and receipt |
|---|---|---|
| Foreground answer | Current private session; no duplicate push while being read | Recheck source/audience before later playback |
| Quiet record | Activity inbox, no sound/badge by default | One item per task/revision; invalidated output unavailable |
| Optional suggestion | Off initially; opt-in digest at a chosen local time | At most one digest/day; suggested 24-hour expiry; no repeat nag after ignoring |
| Requested reminder | In-context OS permission and exact due/destination review | Scheduler commit, OS submission, delivery and acknowledgement remain separate |
| Collaborator update | Inbox first; opt-in generic private cue | Group by conversation; proposed cap two cues/hour, recipient can lower/mute |
| Independent alarm/help | Separate domain policy and qualification | Ordinary quiet settings never disable native independent alarms |

No guessed household bedtime: each user chooses quiet hours or no proactive notifications. “Why this?”, “Mute this kind” and “View later” are consistently available. No response means neither consent nor incapacity. Do not label routine suggestions critical to evade platform interruption controls.

Apple documents contextual authorization, current-setting checks and OS-managed local scheduling [S04–S05]. Native local reminders are therefore a plausible later alternative to an awake PC. This does not prove Haven background execution, delivery timing, cross-device behavior or human receipt. Accepted scheduling is never phrased as guaranteed delivery.

### D4. Accessibility and setup

Propose WCAG 2.2 AA as the web target, not a compliance claim. Relevant criteria include keyboard operation, unobscured focus, non-color cues, reflow, accessible authentication, alternatives to dragging and programmatic status messages. The minimum pointer target criterion is 24×24 CSS pixels with specified exceptions [S01]. Proposed primary Stop/Cancel/Share controls are at least 44 CSS pixels high where practical.

Manual acceptance should cover S1–S4 keyboard and screen-reader completion, source inspection, cancellation, 200% text scaling and 320-CSS-pixel reflow without lost controls, reduced motion, captions/transcripts and editable recognition text. No essential action requires speaking, hearing, color discrimination, dragging or sustained hold. Push-to-talk offers tap-toggle and keyboard alternatives. Authentication supports accessible platform mechanisms; no memory/voice puzzle as the sole route.

Maps/scenes offer object lists and structured descriptions of location, age, known/estimated/unobserved status. W3C complex-image guidance supports short identification plus fuller alternatives [S06]. Replay can pause/step, retains reading position and never steals focus on updates. AR/haptic output is optional. Generated accessible descriptions still require grounding; accessibility does not establish truth or safe navigation.

Proposed onboarding: choose independent profile/session; open synthetic demo or select notebook; choose text/detail; inspect memory controls; complete one sourced question. Target first sourced answer within 5 minutes and at most 5 decisions after application availability. Connecting a provider is separately measured. Neither B's enrollment nor notification/camera/mic/cloud/calendar permission is required for A's first useful task; both users can skip sharing and optional modalities.

## Five scenario cards

**Sequence.** Retrieve current r2, cite date/exact locator and show r1 superseded. “The corrected note chose B because its clamp fits the measured rail. The vibration check remains open.” Follow-up yields an editable three-step plan: verify measurement, compare baseline, record result. “Share the plan with B” previews minimized content, recipient and purpose. The derived plan retains parent dependencies; release requires the current R03 checks, not just A pressing Share.

**Ambiguity/interruption.** Two stand projects yield a narrow safe choice; preserve the question. Resume another device with “A draft plan is ready; nothing was shared.” If r3 arrives mid-answer, invalidate queued text/audio and regenerate. Preference correction is saved only after a revision receipt. “Forget that” resolves the affected preference/note/context; task cancellation is not deletion.

**Accessible/audience behavior.** Short answer, source link and expandable history with the same hierarchy in optional speech. B sees only eligible plan content; search snippets/source titles themselves pass access checks. No shared-speaker fallback. A may privately edit or discard the plan without a notification to B.

**Offline.** Own local notebook use requires the future reviewed local policy and visible revision/last sync; it is not necessarily current. No new private/shared release or replay without current permission verification. Save a local draft while pending sharing remains unavailable.

**Measures, NOT_EXECUTED.** Eight frozen recall questions per pilot user: ≥7 correct current decisions with source; zero unsupported additions/disclosures; all corrections reflected after invalidation. ### S2 — Decision support: use the next hour and learn from failure

**Value/inputs.** B supplies tasks, 60 minutes and shared measurements: a fixed baseline outperformed fixture candidate C. “Retest or revise?” A's calendar/health data are not inputs; no fabrication is requested.

**Sequence.** Offer three concrete options: repeat questionable measurement, draft a one-variable revision, or defer for missing calibration. Distinguish supplied time estimates from measured duration. Explain why another measurement may reduce uncertainty more than another print. Keep failed C, fixed objective/evaluator and baseline visible; link them to a bounded revision proposal. The chosen plan is editable. A conversational choice never accepts a scientific improvement.

**Ambiguity/interruption.** Clarify whether “better” means cost, fit or vibration. Incompatible units/conditions remain separate rather than averaged. Pause at the time budget, save comparison, refresh evidence on resume and show changes to the recommendation. User disagreement is retained without persuasive personality pressure.

**Accessible/audience behavior.** Brief recommendation plus three-row comparison with headers and plain uncertainty; no color-only tradeoff. Only shared results enter A+B view. B can plan privately and decide whether to share. Progress is quiet unless requested.

**Offline.** Frozen results remain comparable with deterministic arithmetic/source excerpts if eligible, even when optional inference is unavailable. Label stale-input recommendations. No live readiness claim, printer queue, robot request or paid worker follows from the plan.

**Measures, NOT_EXECUTED.** Six matched planning cases per user, including retained failure/incompatible measurements: both distinguish measured failure from proposed improvement in every case; evaluator unchanged; failures retained; no fabrication dispatch. Compare worksheet baseline, target ≥5/6 usable edited plans with all stated constraints and no increased task burden. Record disagreements. ### Interruption and ambiguity repair

During assistant playback, ambiguous “Stop” stops playback as a reversible presentation action, then presents separate task/reminder controls. It does not guess a physical action. “Cancel this investigation” addresses that job epoch, suppresses further output, and shows termination observed versus unknown. “Cancel tomorrow's reminder” resolves the exact reminder and durable cancellation. “Request robot recovery” enters the distinct domain path, never cuts power. Contradictory intent asks one specific question and preserves work.

Proposed target: local feedback ≤250 ms and audible cutoff ≤500 ms after an explicit local stop action on a future exact device/runtime. ASR/network timing is separately measured and cannot weaken the local control. Queued speech carries output/audience/grant revisions and expires; cancelled speech never resumes after OS interruption. Apple documents interruption begin/end and conditional resume [S07]; permission to resume audio is not authority to disclose changed private content. Partial drafts remain labeled partial; superseded turns cannot publish old output.
