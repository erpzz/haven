## P2 — Full cost ledger required before procurement

For every row, compute delivered capital cost as the exact selected nonoverlapping component bill plus shipping, tax/import and required setup. Do not add included parts twice. Include: chassis/arm; printed parts and failed prints; fasteners/guarding; controller and host; cameras/lighting; battery/charger/power protection; payload retention tray; independent stop/takeover; network; metrology and calibration fixtures; spare wear parts. Then separately estimate labor, storage/retention, power, consumables, replacement, software updates, calibration repetition, privacy administration, service fees and subscriptions. These dimensions are present but **amounts UNKNOWN** except the source subtotals above.

No model inference or subscription was purchased. P0 requires zero new purchases, paid calls or downloads. Existing-machine electricity and author labor are not measured or claimed free. Later GPU purchase/rental, training data curation and hours per successful retained skill are unknown. A mobile arm's low base price can be dominated by operator/qualification time; this is an engineering concern, not a measured cost claim.

A recorded demonstration must bind capture intervals/clock mapping, camera intrinsics/extrinsics and crop transform, joint ordering/unit/zero/range, actual command receipt and measured state, intervention and success labels, environment/object/task identity, consent/rights/retention and complete upstream closure. Split by session/object arrangement and acquisition lineage, not adjacent video frames. A demonstration is expert behavior under its conditions, not proof every imitated action is safe.

Joint targets, joint deltas, end-effector deltas and velocity commands are different action spaces. Never reinterpret a model's seven-number vector as this robot's motor command. Pin gripper convention, coordinate frame, normalization/denormalization, sample rate, action duration, camera ordering, crop, robot kinematics and latency. Reject mismatches rather than guessing. Chunked/asynchronous inference must carry origin observation and expiry on each chunk; revoke/cancel/track-loss invalidates queued elective actions. A later, apparently fresh chunk cannot extend authority from its stale source.

LeKiwi tutorials include remote host/client control and dataset upload workflows. Future authorized collection must select an explicit local-only profile, disable unapproved telemetry/hub uploads and inspect dependency egress; no tutorial command was copied into execution. A tutorial's physical `replay` can move hardware and must never be confused with R09-P0's no-device replay. [S06]

## P4 — Localization, collision, recovery and the simulation gap

Maintain exact map/odom/base/sensor frames and their R07 semantics. Map relocalization jumps must invalidate pose-dependent goals; odometry continuity does not supply global truth. Unknown scale, drift, stale extrinsics, wheel slip or missing clocks remove applicability. A map's free support describes observed sensor evidence within its coverage, not a permanent route guarantee. Overhangs, glass, low obstacles, stairs, cables, pets and people require task-appropriate coverage and dynamic handling; no such coverage was measured here.

Nav2 Collision Monitor filters commands using sensor-defined zones below the planner, but its authors explicitly deny hard real-time safety certification. Use it only as supplemental candidate software. It cannot replace independent device-local stop/watchdog, physically tested stopping distance, manual takeover or a guarded work area. Exclusion masks, docking modes and stale-input behavior must be reviewed because they can remove protection. [S19]

A physics simulator can support kinematics, workspace coverage, timing and collision hypotheses; contact friction, backlash, cable forces, compliant fingertips, latency, camera exposure, lighting, wheel traction, payload retention and battery sag can differ materially from reality. Randomization is a sensitivity study, not proof the true distribution is covered. Preserve failures, simulator asset/model/seed hashes and independent measurement comparisons. Do not change the original PX4/Gazebo baseline to introduce ground robotics; select a separate ground simulation profile only in a later authorized package.

## P5 — Ambition retained with bounded entry experiments

DIGIT supplies released tactile-sensor manufacturing designs under CC BY-NC4.0, separate from other software. A tactile image is not calibrated force. First compare synthetic contact/loss evidence against explicit UNKNOWN force; later calibrate a selected sensor/apparatus independently before any force claim. [S20]

Stanford's SNAG perching research is a primary author demonstration of bird-inspired grasping/perching on an aerial platform. Its specialized mechanics and impact/grip conditions are not a low-risk extension of a wheeled carrier. First encode perching as a non-executable capability with missing contact/recovery qualifications; keep H05/H10 ownership and a separately authorized apparatus study. [S21]

Event cameras report brightness-change events, with source-specific synchronization. They may be worth comparing in fast motion or difficult lighting, but do not directly establish intensity, object identity or safe collision avoidance. First define a timestamp/threshold/outage replay and compare against an ordinary camera baseline; actual sensor price, SDK/data rights and calibration remain unknown. [S22–S23]

Compliant grippers, tactile feedback, active viewpoints, mobile manipulation and multimodal policies remain future branches. Each must show a task-specific advantage over fixed/manual alternatives at matched cost and intervention burden, with an independently frozen evaluator. None becomes a prerequisite for the first useful replay or ordinary urgent communication.
