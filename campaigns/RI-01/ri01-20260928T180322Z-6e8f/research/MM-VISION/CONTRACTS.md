# Proposed visual evidence profiles

Namespace `ri01.proposed.mmvision.v1`, documentation only. Add to CORE I02/I03/I04 and R07 CR-R07-01; do not replace shared authority, output, geometry or observation contracts. Unsupported profile rejects; no deployed endpoint, schema migration or executor exists here.

## MV-C1 VisualAsset and preprocessing

Producer: selected-media import or later separately admitted capture/decoder. Consumers: bounded perception adapters, R07 admission, MM-FUSION and R15 authorized views.

Required identity: original asset ID/revision, raw-byte SHA256/digest kind, declared MIME and independently checked decoded format, original locator, origin and rights/retention records. A metadata digest cannot stand for unseen raw bytes. Store the original under its authorized retention policy; derivatives never silently replace it. Decoder errors and oversized decoded pixels reject without external content execution. File/URL text in an image is not a fetch instruction.

Dimensions: encoded dimensions/orientation/color profile; normalized decoded dimensions; explicit pixel-coordinate convention (top-left origin, x right/y down, half-open box edges, original pixel edge units); original-to-derived and inverse mapping, crop box, resize scales, padding/letterbox, rotation/EXIF transform, color conversion, codec/decode/normalization versions and output hash. Noninvertible operations declare information loss and supported-region masks. Bounds are validated before mapping. Lens undistortion is a separately versioned transform with calibration and interpolation. A rectified crop does not inherit unchanged camera intrinsics. No normalized-coordinate values may be relabeled as pixels or metres.

Temporal: original capture interval, receipt and process time separately; source clock/boot, native timebase and exact frame PTS, duration/exposure/rolling-shutter profile when known, UTC mapping/offset/drift and uncertainty. Unknown capture time remains unknown; file modification time is not capture time. Video records selected window, policy, every actual sampled PTS and explicit uncovered/dropped/corrupt intervals. Frame ordinal/FPS is insufficient for variable-frame-rate clips. Exact decode/edit/resample lineage binds an extracted frame to source bytes. Static-image EXIF is a reported field, not authenticated time.

Every object carries CORE's complete current AuthorityVector and full transitive influencing closure by exact immutable reference, not a subset containing only audience. Preserve source revision/hash/lineage/authenticity epochs, principal/session/device, grants, subject/participant-area, policy/purpose/rights, tool/service catalog, job/lease/cancel, destination/route/audience/provider, authority/restore and canonicalization dependencies. A stored vector is a snapshot; current eligibility is rechecked at retrieval, context construction, egress, release and consumption. Crop/mask selection does not remove parent capture obligations.

## MV-C2 VisualObservation

Envelope: unique observation ID/revision; exact C1 parent(s), transform/model/runtime/tokenizer/prompt/preprocessing/configuration hashes, full influence refs (including uncited context, calibration, prior, track memory and embedding source), original region/frame/time support, estimator profile, raw score and score kind, calibration evidence or UNKNOWN, failure/OOD/abstention status, correlation group/dependence edges, and CORE/R07 metadata. Scores from unlike models are never averaged as probabilities without a validated calibration/fusion profile.

Typed payloads:

- Detection: class vocabulary/revision, candidate class alternatives, original-space box, detector threshold and raw score, selected-instance reference and ambiguity. Missing detection means no supported detection under this profile, not absence of object.
- Segmentation: binary/probabilistic mask encoding/shape/threshold, original-space support, prompt point/box references and author, holes/occlusion/unknown boundaries, mask revision. Masks may overlap; a filled mask is not observed material behind an occluder.
- OCR: original polygon and ordered character/word spans, raw transcription, normalization as a distinct derivative, language/script hypotheses and recognition scores. Unreadable is explicit. Preserve ambiguous digits, punctuation and units; task-critical transcription requires pixel verification or abstention. Embedded imperatives, QR and URLs stay data.
- Anonymous track: clip/session-scoped ID, ordered observation refs and PTS, association method/threshold, confidence semantics, branches, occluded/missing spans, start/expiry/restart and termination reason. A track without a current observation is an extrapolation. Reappearance can start a new ID. No principal/health-subject identity is derived from a track; no cross-camera linkage without a separately qualified authorized profile.
- Geometry: image-only bearing/relative depth or camera/scene estimate tagged RELATIVE/UNKNOWN until independent scale evidence. Metric geometry carries exact R07 frame/map, paired SpatialSemantics ID/hash/version, calibration validity, units, scale evidence and uncertainty convention. Preserve camera-from-world inversion and original convention explicitly; class score is not covariance. No direct safe-route/free-space/physical-command claim.
- Change: paired eligible source revisions/time intervals, registration method/quality, common visible support, change region and alternative illumination/camera-motion explanations. Uncovered or occluded areas remain unknown. A detector vocabulary change must not masquerade as a scene change.
- Retrieval match: query and candidate source revisions, embedding model/preprocessing, similarity metric/raw score, eligibility check, top-k rank and uncertainty. Metadata filtering and current rights precede exposure of candidate snippets/counts; recheck before context/release. Retrieval is a lead, not corroboration or identity.
- VLM claim: exact original/region/time references and eligible textual parents, claim text/type, cited versus influencing refs, uncertainty/contradiction/abstention and validation result. A citation's presence is not entailment. A proposed label remains an estimator claim, not observed ground truth.

`claim_class` distinguishes supplied source observation, estimator output, inferred hypothesis, generated illustration, qualified measurement and historical replay. These axes do not overwrite R07 support state OBSERVED_OCCUPIED/OBSERVED_FREE/OCCLUDED/UNOBSERVED/CONFLICT. Ordinary RGB pixels alone do not qualify thermal sensing; pseudocolor styling is marked visualization. An apparent empty mask cannot change unobserved support to observed-free. This profile excludes private-person identification, protected-trait or emotion inference from appearance, medical diagnosis, covert surveillance and weapons targeting. A VLM's ability to emit such labels is not an eligible capability.

## MV-C3 Temporal and cross-modal seam

Actual peer Q-MMV-AV-01/Q-MMAV-01 is recorded in CONSULTATIONS.md. MM-AV contributes audio sample-rate/channel/index ranges, clock and AV offset method/uncertainty, while MV contributes original frame PTS/exposure and visual gaps. Both preserve stream and shared-capture correlation IDs. Emit OBSERVED_AT_SAMPLE and INFERRED_BETWEEN_SAMPLES separately. Audio suggesting a dropped object does not become a visually observed between-frame event; emit UNKNOWN_VISUAL_BETWEEN_SAMPLES with separate audio-supported hypothesis. Overlapping uncertain event intervals do not support strict before/after order. Speaker/track IDs confer no identity or recipient authority.

R07 receives admitted evidence with original/derived refs, typed uncertainty, correlation and exact geometry dependencies. It may keep conflicting outputs separate or select one declared estimate; MV does not shrink covariance by treating caption + OCR + crop + track as independent witnesses. Repeated frames share estimator/prior/calibration history. MM-FUSION owns any later calibrated fusion; no author approval is implied by this proposal.

R15 view request binds source/map revisions, selected valid time and knowledge cutoff, principal/purpose/destination and complete current vector. Overlay toggles expose source-time, measured/estimated/generated classes and unknown extent, with click-through to currently eligible original regions. Redacted parents must not leak geometry, text, counts, heatmaps, thumbnails or embeddings. Map views keep `supports_safe_route_claim=false`. Generated enhancement is a separate illustrative layer with its own hash and never replaces the original.

R04 selected existing media is first. Direct camera, background video, microphone and cloud transfer are separate operation grants/capabilities. Exact native device/OS/SDK/app tuple and stop/capture status follow W01/W03. A process stop or unavailable output does not establish sensor cessation. Wearer uncertainty is not resolved with face matching. R09/R10 only receive typed observation/proposal refs; perception never directly issues motion or dispatch.

## MV-C4 Operations, bounds, correction and output

Proposed `derive_visual_evidence(exact_refs, profile, budget)` is pure bounded computation; same immutable key/digest returns history, conflicting digest rejects. One retry maximum only for a pure eligible task within original reservation/deadline. Unknown external dispatch/acquisition/output is reconciled read-only. No arbitrary shell/URL/file executor. Output requires CORE I06's four orthogonal records and finite-use whole-output/single-destination profile; a new replay/route is a new admission, no double charge or reminted slot.

Initial selected-image profile: one original ≤12 megapixels and ≤16MiB encoded, at most two derived crops and 4 megapixels total model-input pixels, one selected instance/question, ≤32 evidence sources and existing CORE closure bounds, one active inference attempt, ≤512 generated tokens, original job deadline60s. These are proposed allocation limits, not tested capacity. Decoder may need a lower implementation-specific safe cap; oversize rejects, never silently downscales beyond a declared transform or truncates authority closure. No clip processing in this initial profile.

Reusable errors: SCHEMA_INVALID, UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE, HASH_MISMATCH, CLOCK_UNKNOWN, STALE, FRAME_UNRESOLVED, CALIBRATION_INVALID, SCALE_UNKNOWN, CORRELATION_UNMODELED, LINEAGE_INCOMPLETE, SCOPE_DENIED, CONSENT_REVOKED, OOD, RESOURCE_BUSY, BUDGET_EXHAUSTED, CANCELLED, OUTCOME_UNKNOWN. Proposed visual refinements: DECODE_FAILED, DECODE_LIMIT, REGION_AMBIGUOUS, TEXT_UNREADABLE, TEMPORAL_SUPPORT_GAP. Public errors must not disclose restricted parent details.

Correction/tombstone immediately denies source and all derivatives: masks/crops/OCR, track history, embeddings/search candidates, contexts, queued/rejected outputs, rendered tiles, forks and caches. If compressed state cannot remove influence, deny the entire affected state and rebuild only from eligible parents. Retain separate per-copy requested/acknowledged/verified-erased/unknown statuses under approved retention; hashes/IDs are linkable and not exempt. Restore begins review-only under new epochs. GPU state and decoder buffers need their own erasure/cessation observations; document-only invalidation is not proof of erasure.
