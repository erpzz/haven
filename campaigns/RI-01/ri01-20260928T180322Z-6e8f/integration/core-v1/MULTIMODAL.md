## Two distinct capability matrices

Agent-tool evidence this turn: text/JSON source reading, hashing, structural checks and document writing only. No direct image/audio/video inspection, model execution, telemetry acquisition, sensing or device control. VISION-0's direct media check and other reviewers' scientific figure access belong to their attributed agents; they do not establish this agent's access or future Haven quality.
