# Full multimodal design and evaluation

This document covers Haven's future product architecture separately from the research tools used to design it. None of these proposed protocols ran in RI-01. MODALITY_MATRIX.md gives the actual tool inspection limits; SOURCE_USE_INDEX.json attributes observations to their real inspectors. Direct audio and video were TOOL_UNAVAILABLE; discovered decoders were not exercised. Image/PDF smoke observations demonstrate a research workflow, not Haven runtime perception. Generation is never observation.

## Useful first path and retained breadth

The first useful behavior is one selected original image, one typed question and currently eligible supporting notes, yielding an answer tied to the actual region or source. For a blue box with a partly unreadable label, answer the supported color and mark the uncertain character. Do not invent distance, current presence or fit. Offer a permitted closer image only when it helps. Ordinary source lookup and deterministic extraction remain useful baselines; future models must add measured semantic utility.

The retained system handles text, code, documents and diagram context; visual objects, segmentation, temporary anonymous tracking and changes; selected clips, events and occlusion; push-to-talk speech, ASR, acoustic events and diarization without identity; depth, pose, occupancy, spatial and geospatial data; RF, CSI, RSSI, ranging, radar, clocks, IMU and telemetry; consented health-adjacent measures and later muscle, eye and neural-interface research. No text model is presumed to interpret arbitrary raw signals or diagnose a person.

Outputs include grounded text and structured records, explanatory plots and diagrams, speech, scene overlays and AR, maps/3D/time-series views, private deliberate haptic cues, and typed digital or physical proposals. Generated media remains an illustration or hypothesis. Output channels have distinct audiences; the first finite profile permits one whole output at one destination. Audio plus captions, streaming, recurrence and multiple destinations require new qualified profiles. A cue accepted by a device is not a cue perceived by a person.

## A small shared architecture

Acquisition/import permission -> source and native time/frame/quality normalization -> qualified specialist perception -> typed evidence and uncertainty -> current scoped retrieval and compatibility checks -> late fusion -> reasoning or proposal -> current publication/domain authority -> separately observed outcome.

Reuse CORE I01-I07 and all native R07/MM-VISION/MM-AV/R14 fields. A concise envelope does not replace those fields. Keep original bytes and locators (page/span/pixel polygon/frame PTS/audio samples/native measurement range), raw identity separate from canonical metadata identity, transforms, artifact/runtime/preprocessing provenance, rights and retention. Retention does not mean indefinite raw storage: ORIGINAL_UNAVAILABLE is explicit if later rereading is required but no longer eligible or possible. Bounded file/object assets plus existing SQLite metadata are sufficient candidates; no new mandatory service is introduced.

Evidence distinguishes observed, inferred, historical, generated, simulated, stylized and reference classes separately from support state. Occupied, free, occluded, unobserved and conflict are not interchangeable. Claim class, uncertainty, provenance and support survive rendering and export. A scene cannot turn simulated motion into observed motion or thermal styling into measured temperature.

## Alignment, fusion and memory

Preserve capture support, receipt, processing and knowledge times; native integer clock ticks/sample indices and rational timebase; boot and clock-mapping revisions; drift/offset uncertainty and uncovered intervals. PTS is presentation support, not proven UTC capture. Frames cannot establish events between exposures, and transcripts cannot establish prosody or speaker identity. Operation-specific AlignmentDecision records accepted, refused or unknown compatibility. A_BEFORE_B requires strict separation after uncertainty propagation; overlap gives ORDER_UNKNOWN, and separation does not establish causation.

Spatial joins retain frame/map/CRS, axes/handedness, horizontal and vertical datum, scale, calibration and exact R06/R07 SpatialSemantics: parent-from-child transform, normalized xyzw quaternion, parent-frame left perturbation, covariance order and units. Unknown location stays UNKNOWN/null. Relative scale is not metres. R08's first exact scalar adapter is not a pose/covariance adapter. Unsupported conversions fail or omit the dependent claim; a declared future rounding rule is not permission to weaken R08's exact first profile.

The initial late-fusion baseline compares eligible typed claims, preserves disagreement, and returns supported facts with bounded unknowns. A crop, OCR, caption and VLM answer share their source; nearby frames, models and calibration can share errors. Unknown dependence forbids independent-weight multiplication. Select a qualified estimate or show alternatives when no joint statistical model exists. Early fusion remains a task-specific comparator requiring paired eligible data, actual synchronization, missing-modality handling and independently protected evidence; superiority is not established here.

Permission filtering precedes ranking, counts, context and retrieval. Use current exact source revisions and full influencing AuthorityVector, including uncited dependencies. Tombstones/corrections invalidate summaries, embeddings, tracks, tiles, caches, adaptation state and queued output. Incomplete reverse lineage denies affected materialization. Per-copy requested, acknowledged, verified erased and unknown are separate; inaccessible is not erased. Scene import restores presentation intent only. Authority/evidence persistence rollback invokes the separate new-epoch review-only restore procedure.

## Budgets, cancellation and delivery

Reserve from one parent allocation, use exact attempt/worker/boot/fence identities and deadlines, and count failed, partial, cancelled, retry and tuning work in the same inclusive ceiling. Unknown admitted exposure remains unsettled. No fallback escalates authority or spends a new provider budget. Preserve interactive output and cancellation capacity between users; numerical fairness claims require measurement.

Capture stop, compute stop, playback stop, durable recurrence cancellation and physical recovery are separate requests and observations. Release/consume commits, delivery history and current eligibility remain separate. Lost ACK cannot prove nondelivery; a flush or hidden UI cannot prove remote buffers stopped or human perception did not occur. Only fresh current checks can release a new private result. Public offline usefulness can survive Internet loss; loss of required authoritative state denies dependent private output.

## Richer simultaneous journey and staged additions

A later separately authorized replay combines two camera exposures, a short audio interval, region-scoped RF, IMU and an older calibrated map. Asked whether an object moved before a sound and whether a passage is clear, Haven can identify visible objects while reporting unknown event order if intervals overlap. RF silence and occlusion cannot clear the passage. A shared frame/crop/caption is one dependent origin. A frame or calibration conflict remains explicit. The system can propose a finite stationary observation; rover or aircraft movement needs separate domain qualification. Urgent native help remains useful independently of every carrier.

Sequence: inert useful evidence composition; selected-image model comparison only after lifecycle, decoder, artifact and oracle gates; separate amended ASR-only study; later clips and telemetry with their own original assets and clocks; then richer simultaneous and observatory work. Retained muscle/gaze/haptic, engineering, ground/air and cooperative Earth-scale branches remain in the original scope matrix. A selected image slice does not claim to implement them.

Accepted MM-FUSION F08 and R15 projection/export form the reconciled presentation seam. A digest-bound sidecar must preserve essential source class, support, native time/frame/calibration, uncertainty, derivation and current closure. Unsupported/lossy consumers omit affected geometry with an unavailable table or reject UNSUPPORTED_PROFILE. The accepted R15 CR01-06 profile conserves this seam; its first export is one bounded public JSON document with an embedded digest-bound sidecar.
