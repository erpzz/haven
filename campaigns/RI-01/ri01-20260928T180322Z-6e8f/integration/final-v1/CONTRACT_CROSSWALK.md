R08's first admitted scalar profile supersedes earlier provisional rounding advice for its narrow quantities: exact conversion only. Broader unit/spatial adapters need explicit dimensions, frames, datums, covariance and error budgets. R07/R10 retain native time, altitude datum, boot and calibration. Publication, egress, sensing, digital help and motion retain separate authority even when they share dependencies.

Current-code gaps remain separate from these design decisions. No schema, application source, database or adapter was executed or migrated. SOURCE_USE_INDEX maps findings to changed sections and rejected alternatives; INPUT_USE pins complete accepted composites rather than original reports alone.
