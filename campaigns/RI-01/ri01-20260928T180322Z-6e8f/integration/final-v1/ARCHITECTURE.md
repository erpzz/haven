## A useful personal system that can grow

Haven helps two independently consenting people understand their records and surroundings, plan useful work, and learn from outcomes. The first useful path is a selected original image, a typed question and current authorized notes, producing a source-linked grounded answer and a resolved follow-up. “Which fixture is this?” can get a useful appearance or label answer while “will it fit?” waits for qualified dimensions. A lack of metric scale must not force refusal of supported nonmetric facts.

The same evidence structure supports everyday recall and editable plans, scoped digital tools, speech and clips, wearable observations, uncertain world maps, public regional inquiry, low-force robotics, resilient communications and measured engineering. These are staged branches of one retained vision, not a requirement to deploy a fleet before answering a question. All sixteen vision anchors and five journeys are detailed in VISION_COVERAGE.md. RETAINED_SCOPE.json preserves every original REQ, AT and EX record plus per-original next work and evidence gaps.

Keep the established Python 3.12 / FastAPI / Pydantic 2 / Jinja2 / one Uvicorn worker / SQLite context. The original M4 `qwen2.5vl:3b` advisory pin stays unchanged; exact selected artifact rights, runtime and containment remain unqualified. Logical components can remain in-process. Team roles do not imply a service or model per agent. No new broker, mandatory vector database, universal agent framework or rewrite is justified by this research.

## Data and control flow

Explicit acquisition/import → original artifact and clock/frame/quality normalization → task-specific perception → typed evidence with uncertainty → current authorized retrieval and conservative late fusion → bounded reasoning or deterministic projection → validated output/domain proposal → operation-specific current authority → observed outcome.

Store original assets as appropriate files/objects and references/indexes in the existing application where sufficient. A caption, crop, OCR result and source frame retain shared ancestry. Do not flatten video, RF or maps into untraceable prose or feed every sample into a frontier context. Begin with lexical/metadata/geometry retrieval and typed late fusion; compare embeddings or richer fusion only against a useful measured baseline. Missing/conflicting modalities stay visible. Correction/revocation invalidates all influencing summaries, embeddings, tracks, tiles, forks and caches, including uncited inputs.

## Daily, multimodal and domain behavior

Daily: current corrected notes, short editable plans and local drafts work before OAuth. R05's finite seven-day agenda projection can later use one already-used provider, exact account/calendar/window and bounded read-only projection. Partial pages are partial coverage; a free-looking slot is not proof a person is available. External writes use endpoint-specific sealed payload and reconciliation profiles. Lookup failure, missing ACK and successful current absence cannot prove that no earlier write occurred. Recurrence is a separate bounded profile with stable occurrence identity, timezone/rules/DST policy and fresh authority per occurrence.

Wearables: phone-selected still/private response first, then synthetic measurement chronology and native tuple qualification. HealthKit read visibility is intentionally opaque; Haven withdrawal is its own authoritative state change, not a guaranteed OS Settings callback. Historical measurements are not current condition. Camera, audio, display, gestures and haptics are separately qualified. Private route loss pauses output. Deliberate muscle/gaze/button input selects a proposal; it does not authenticate a person, mint consent or dispatch motion.

World model: preserve observed, inferred, historical and unknown support with exact frame/map/calibration and SpatialSemantics. Missing returns do not establish free space. A map never becomes a safe-route certificate. Local replay is useful without Earth alignment. Active perception proposes one eligible next observation under a finite total episode budget; sensing permission does not authorize carrier motion.

Engineering: keep baseline, design/process/specimen identity, negative and inconclusive results, correction history and protected evaluator. R08-P00's accepted first extension is inert exact scalar/correction handling. One-axis reseating does not establish 6DoF, RF neutrality, contact force or task benefit. CAD, fabrication, inspection, qualification, handling and cooling remain distinct.

Embodiment: compare fixed/parked sensing before ground carriage or flight. Teleoperation and task-specific demonstrations precede learned skills. Unknown payload/gripper/pose/stop retains conflicting exclusion. Generic motor-off can drop a load; recovery is apparatus-specific. EC120 compatibility remains unknown; DJI support is exact model/SDK/firmware dependent. Complete costs include control, power, cameras, guarding, spares, labor and rights, not just a component subtotal.

Communications/help: pre-reviewed public help and ordinary native help are useful independently of AI, aircraft and map quality. Local storage, relay, backhaul, destination application, device presentation, human acknowledgement and professional response are separate facts. Internet loss need not mean loss of a complete trusted local authority; unresolved authority still denies new private release. Encryption does not create permission. No emergency contact or physical response is initiated by this synthesis.

The accepted MM-FUSION and corrected R15 composites are incorporated as additive evidence and presentation profiles. Their useful target is inspectable recorded evidence, honest fog of war, selectable owned resources, isolated scenario forks, capability progression and cooperative public-world work. Scenario import into an intact authority store is untrusted scene data; restoring/rolling back authority/evidence state is the distinct new-epoch review-only operation.
