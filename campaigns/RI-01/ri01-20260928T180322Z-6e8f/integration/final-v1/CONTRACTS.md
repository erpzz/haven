# Shared contracts — final synthesis candidate v1

Documentation namespace `ri01.proposed.core.v1`; no deployed wire schema or migration. Accepted upstream composites are pinned in INPUT_USE.json. Unknown is explicit with a reason; never coerce it to zero, false, NOT_SENT, safe or successful. Reject unsupported versions/profiles, nonfinite numbers, unknown enum values and incomplete dependency closure. Opaque IDs and digests identify records; they never grant authority.

## I01 AccessSnapshot and AuthorityVector

Principal, subject, steward, rights-holder, audience, sponsor, service and device are distinct. Consent does not imply license, ownership, clinical authority or action rights. Joint-source eligibility is the intersection of every influencing source/grant, including uncited context, cached intermediates and derived parents. Operations remain typed: read, retain, derive, share, cloud-transmit, audible-output, emergency-disclose; publication, egress, digital write, sensing and physical dispatch have distinct ordering points. No operation grants the others.

The complete vector is: `vector_version`; `authority_instance_epoch`, `restore_epoch`; `policy_ref`, `purpose_definition_ref`, `rights_policy_refs[]`; `principal_ref`, `session_revision`, `device_binding_revision`, `device_epoch`; `service_capability_revision`, `tool_catalog_digest`; `grant_dependencies[{id,revision,revocation_epoch}]`; `source_dependencies[{id,revision,sha256,lineage_epoch,authenticity_epoch}]`; `subject_scope_revision`, `participant_area_scope_revision`; `job_ref`, `lease_fence`, `cancel_scope_epochs[{scope_id,epoch}]`; `destination_ref`, `destination_epoch`, `audience_revision`, `route_profile_revision`; `provider_data_policy_revision` (explicit NOT_APPLICABLE for local); `dependency_closure_digest`, `canonicalization_version`.

Use full dependency equality/current preconditions, never one global scalar epoch. Also check operation/purpose, expiry and time uncertainty. A vector is a captured snapshot, not a reusable bearer permission. Anonymous RF uses area/session scope without inferred identity. P0 bounds: two synthetic people, 32 sources, 16 required grants, 128 lineage edges, depth 16, metadata 64 KiB and output 256 KiB. Reject oversized/incomplete closure rather than truncating it. These are the R03 proposed laboratory limits, not production scale claims.

## I02 SourceRecord and DerivedEvidence; I03 ContextSnapshot

SourceRecord carries versioned ID/revision/raw digest, digest kind, media type, origin/claim class, original locator, capture/sample interval, receive time, processing time, source clock/boot and UTC-mapping uncertainty, derivation parents/transforms, rights/retention/access dependencies, quality/freshness and correction/tombstone links. A metadata canonical digest is distinct from a raw-byte hash. Locators distinguish image region, video frame/interval, audio interval, document span and spatial observation. Generated illustration, hypothesis, replay, supplied observation and qualified measurement retain distinct claim classes.

ContextSnapshot adds request/job/packet, complete access and source/memory revision vectors, bounded excerpts/media references, omissions/conflicts, capability catalog digest, route, aggregate byte/token/frame/audio/resource limits, expiry and cancel epochs. Recheck before retrieval, provider egress and release. A packet cannot declassify source data. Memory correction uses expected-revision compare-and-swap and a commit receipt; every derivative/cache/index follows lineage invalidation. The lineage index cannot be an eventually consistent security boundary.

Deletion immediately denies further eligibility through a tombstone; per-copy propagation is tracked independently across request/context/raw rejected output, attempt/time/backend metadata, caches, indexes, exports, providers and backups. Hashes and minimized receipts may remain linkable. Inaccessible is not verified erased. Restore creates new authority/restore epochs, starts review-only, and reconciles trusted revocations/tombstones or quarantines data. No jobs, timers, credentials or permits silently resume.

## I04 BoundedJob and AdapterResult

Bind immutable payload digest, idempotency key, sponsor/domain, output contract, exact inputs, reservation, deadline/elapsed bound, attempt, worker-instance, boot, ownership scope, nonce, lease/fence, cancellation scope/epoch, result certainty and exact model/runtime/template/tokenizer/preprocessing identity. PID alone is not identity. All attempts count against remaining budgets. Pure recomputation retries only with fresh eligibility and remaining deadline/budget; unknown external writes never automatically replay.

Compute state is independent: NOT_STARTED, RUNNING, STOP_REQUESTED, STOP_CONFIRMED, UNKNOWN. Independent observed termination is required for STOP_CONFIRMED. Wrapper exit, publication denial or cancellation intent cannot prove a shared/remote provider stopped or ceased billing. Unknown completion is reconciled against exact immutable identity; fenced late results cannot publish. R1 lifecycle tests remain separately required before any worker-termination guarantee.

## I05 Monetary/resource reservation and settlement

Admission atomically reserves conservative worst-case exposure against principal/job/resource and parent allocation. Bind rate revision, integer micro-USD ceilings plus source precision, model calls/tokens/media/time/memory caps, and confirmed/estimated/unknown usage. Sum exact rational cost before upward integer rounding; preserve source units/categories and rounding decision. Duplicate settlements are idempotent; conflicting duplicates reject. Child allocation cannot create capacity.

Timeout, cancellation or expiry does not refund admitted work of unknown usage. Only never-admitted reservations expire unused. Financial/resource reservation is distinct from finite grant-use accounting below. R08 machine/material allowances extend typed resource identities; they are not a second independent model budget.

## I06 Four orthogonal output records

The original combined publication-state suggestion and early Q-CORE-01 enum are superseded by the accepted R02-P01 amendment.

| Record | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ReleaseAuthorizationReceipt | Immutable successfully committed admission of exact output/destination/vector. RELEASE_UNKNOWN is a separate appended observation requiring read-only reconciliation; no send on unknown commit. |
| ConsumptionPermitReceipt | Immutable committed redemption of one exact authorized permit slot, output or chunk, destination/session, nonce/sequence and complete vector. A returned historical receipt does not mint another usable permit. |
| DeliveryObservation | Append-only observations on that exact tuple: NOT_SENT, SENT_UNACKNOWLEDGED, DISPLAY_REPORTED, PLAYBACK_REPORTED, STOP_REPORTED, SUPPRESSED, DELIVERY_UNKNOWN. Reports name authenticated reporter/time/uncertainty; authentication does not prove human perception. |
| PermitEligibilityDecision | Current ELIGIBLE_AT_CHECK, DENIED or UNKNOWN with fresh vector/check time/reason. It changes future eligibility, never historical receipts or playback. It is not bearer authority. |

All four bind output digest/canonicalization; explicit WHOLE_OUTPUT or chunk manifest/ID/index/digest; exact destination/route/audience revisions; release/consume sequences and full AuthorityVector. A short authoritative write transaction (SQLite BEGIN IMMEDIATE is the laboratory candidate) compares complete current dependencies and writes quota claims/outbox/receipt together. No network, model or device operation occurs inside it. Commit failure/uncertainty permits no send. Online consumption freshly checks eligibility; offline mode cannot make a new private/shared release or replay without current verification. Already exported information remains historical and may be unrecallable.

Missing ACK is DELIVERY_UNKNOWN or SENT_UNACKNOWLEDGED, never proof of NOT_SENT. SUPPRESSED requires positive evidence that a future, unconsumed, unsent operation was prevented; in-flight output may already be disclosed. Revocation does not erase a previous DISPLAY_REPORTED/PLAYBACK_REPORTED observation. Cancel/revoke between consume and physical display retains a residual race; record it explicitly rather than promising recall. Private output cannot silently move to a speaker or lock screen.

### P0 finite-use profile

`ONE_RELEASE_ONE_DESTINATION_WHOLE_OUTPUT` counts one successfully committed release admission, bound to whole-output digest, exact destination/route/audience/vector and one consume slot. It does not count reads, tokens, delivery, human perception or consume requests. P0 rejects finite-use chunked or multiple-destination profiles. The general output identity above preserves future fixed-manifest streaming; that extension is deferred, not deleted.

For each applicable finite grant, atomically create one immutable GrantUseClaim, deduplicated by exact grant/profile across the complete source closure. All required grants charge together or none do. A claim binds grant ID/authorization revision/revocation epoch, counted-operation profile, units=1, release operation/key/request digest/receipt ID, output digest/canonicalization, destination/route/audience, full vector/source closure, authority/restore epochs, commit sequence/time/expiry, consume-slot and quota ledger transaction/sequence. Grant `authorization_revision` changes for scope/max-uses/expiry or other authority edits. QuotaLedger `accounting_sequence` changes on charge; charging does not change authorization revision and invalidate its own claim.

A new release requires remaining quota at least one and charges once. A known existing claim redeems its slot only after fresh grant/expiry/source/device/session/audience/policy/cancel/lease/restore checks. It does not require still-unused parent quota or charge that parent again. Consume only transitions the one slot. An exhausted max_uses=1 grant can therefore redeem its already charged valid claim once, but cannot create a second release.

Same release key/request returns immutable history without charge or send. Conflicting tuples reject. Duplicate consumption returns history, not another usable permission. Unknown commit requires read-only reconciliation, no new key/refund. A lost consumed-slot reply does not reset/remint it. Abandonment, expiry, revocation and deletion never automatically refund. Explicit replay is a new operation with new charge and fresh authority; new grants are never silently minted. No negative quota or refunds in P0.

Required traces: useful max_uses=1 release then consume; two concurrent new admissions cannot overdraw; duplicate release/consume; unknown release commit; lost consume reply; revoke/expire between release and consume; shared grant deduplication; all-or-none joint grants; played output then cancel; lost ACK then revoke; wrong output/destination; explicit replay; restored epoch mismatch. These are proposed tests, not executed results.

## I07 DomainProposal and ObservedOutcome

Versioned typed capability/resource, immutable payload, input/qualification references, expected revision, deadline, separate domain authorization, idempotency/attempt, attributed acknowledgements/observations and reconciliation state. No generic shell/SQL/URL/flight/printer executor. Account tokens stay in a scoped broker, outside workers/model context; person/account/issuer/audience scopes cannot inherit employer authority. Physical proposals require their own sealed job, boot/config, supervision, qualification and resource lease. Unknown dispatch is not replayable. Sensing is separate from carrier motion; digital-help eligibility is independent of physical execution.

## Spatial, scalar and aircraft adapters — later accepted closure below

## Final accepted domain reconciliations

**R01 composite:** “Saved here; not sent” requires durable exact local commit AND authoritative LOCAL_ONLY/NOT_DISPATCHED proof, no admitted/issued/buffered/in-flight transport and a closed/fenced no-dispatch boundary. Missing rows, ACKs, timeout or opaque errors preserve UNKNOWN. Read-only reconciliation is itself currently authorized; it never resends or remints. A late matching authenticated report appends history without proving perception. This supersedes looser initial UI wording.

**R08-P00 accepted scalar/time closure:** retain raw decimal mm and exact canonical m with dimension/frame/axis/direction, zero offset, calibration/reference and fixture/material/lot/orientation/process/assembly/enclosure/acquisition/environment revisions. The first profile rejects full pose/covariance and ambiguous scalar datum/frame; opaque source may be retained without promoting the value. Standard/expanded length uncertainty scales by 0.001; dimensionless coverage factor does not. Decimal precision bounds do not claim measurement accuracy. Seconds-to-integer-ms is exact; sub-ms, nonfinite, unknown, overflow and lossy admission reject. Zero permits no positive work; the representational seven-day range does not override the 600000ms run cap. No silent rounding in this first accepted profile. Future covariance uses explicit DΣDᵀ under separately frozen semantics/tolerances, never vector relabeling. Shared reservation/lease/attempt references avoid duplicate budgets. E01 method and physical gates remain open.

**R05:** local/domain attempt and provider observations are separate from I06. A UI permit does not authorize provider mutation. An ordinary restart may preserve facts only if the store is proven intact/current; every occurrence still needs full current checks. Successful DB open is not proof against rollback. Uncertain intactness quarantines. Backup/import/rollback of authority/evidence creates new epochs and review-only state. A separately versioned recurring profile must bound end/count, payload, destinations, total exposure, quiet hours/catch-up and revocation; it cannot reuse one consumed whole-output claim. Complete no-current-match, incomplete search, authentication/authorization errors, ambiguous404, throttle, server and transport errors remain distinct and none proves historical non-dispatch.

**R07/MM-VISION/MM-AV:** mandatory semantics pair ID/hash/version; explicit transform/inverse and pixel/crop/scale mapping, native stream PTS/rational timebase, DTS separate, audio sample ranges/rate/channel/resample/filter delay and gaps. Capture/receive/process time remain separate. Anonymous track/speaker is not a principal or health subject. Correlated priors/calibration/history do not justify independent uncertainty shrinkage. Without a qualified joint model retain alternatives or choose one declared estimate. No audio fills an unseen visual gap. Class scores are not covariance. Current-authority replay is required even for historical query; unverifiable contribution removal blocks/rebuilds affected compressed materialization rather than claiming erasure.

**R04/R14:** native capability binds exact SKU/OS/firmware/region/entitlement and enrollment separately for each person. Signal evidence, intended action, confirmation and authority are distinct. Capture/compute/playback/schedule cancellation and physical recovery remain independently observed outcomes. Requested haptic/SDK success cannot prove perception. Single finite whole-output first profile remains; streaming/bilateral/multiple-destination channels need separately reviewed profiles. Own-grant withdrawal fences new eligibility even where provider read status is opaque.

**R09/R10/R11-public:** sealed physical job binds embodiment/payload/workspace/boot/config/qualification/ownership/exclusion/recovery. Expiry or lost ACK never clears uncertain occupied space. Ordinary help does not depend on robotic success. Receipt layers distinguish local/relay/backhaul/application/presentation/human/professional response. Untrusted scene import into intact current authority is different from restoring its state. No permission is manufactured by encrypted storage, public visibility, nearby signals or a receipt identifier.

R14 personalized classifiers/models and all modal derivatives remain in deletion/retention lineage. Denial, deletion request, acknowledgement, verified removal and unknown are separate. Linkable hashes/metadata are not exempt audit retention. Host-administrator confidentiality is not promised by two application profiles.
