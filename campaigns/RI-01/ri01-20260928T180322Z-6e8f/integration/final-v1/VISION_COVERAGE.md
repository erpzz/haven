## Journey 1 — return to a decision

A asks why the team chose stand B. Haven retrieves the corrected note, marks the older revision superseded, cites the measured rail constraint and states that the vibration check remains open. It offers an editable three-step plan. B sees only deliberately shared eligible evidence. A correction during queued output triggers current revalidation; changing devices restores eligible task state without replaying an old effect. The canonical cross-device AT remains outstanding: R05 account-switch cases provide only partial support.

## Journey 2 — recognize the selected fixture and follow up

A selects an original bench image and asks which fixture is visible. Haven highlights the relevant region and gives supported label/appearance evidence, with uncertain OCR characters exposed. “Will that fit?” resolves the referent and retrieves current permitted dimension records. No scale produces a precise missing-evidence explanation and a proposed measurement, while useful nonmetric information remains available. Original image, crop and caption keep one shared lineage. This is the smallest real-model usefulness target after lifecycle/adapter/artifact/oracle gates.

## Journey 3 — replay a multisensor event

A and B inspect a permitted recorded interval with image/video, audio, RF and telemetry. A timeline separates sample support, missing intervals and uncertain clock alignment. A correction changes the current reconstruction while “known then” remains historical. Conflicting sources remain separately inspectable; correlated crops/captions do not count as independent votes. A useful next-view proposal names expected information and remaining total budget, without motion authority. Rich simultaneous replay is a real future study, not a claim that first image work already implements it.

## Journey 4 — a regional question in Observatory

B asks about a public environmental region. Haven shows source date, resolution, rights, geography and unknown coverage, with an accessible table beside the map. An isolated fork compares hypothetical observation or relay sites; it cannot inherit live device capability or stale private grants. A shared field pin and a private cue have separate audiences. The proposed composed frontend consumes accepted R15/MM-FUSION inputs; a thin two-dimensional view remains a useful fallback. Public visibility never automatically permits unrestricted collection or publication.

## Journey 5 — learn from a failed engineering candidate

A fixed baseline beats candidate C. Haven keeps C and its reasons visible, distinguishes checker rejection from a measured failure, and proposes one variable to change or one missing measurement to resolve. Exact material, lot, orientation, process, calibration and reference revisions travel with the result. A correction appends history and invalidates current applicability without erasing the original. No writer-authored score, review agreement or one-axis repeatability claim becomes fabrication or physical qualification.

Accessibility is coequal with utility: keyboard/text alternatives, source-linked object/event lists, non-color status, pause/step, captions and editable recognition text. Proposed WCAG/native targets remain untested. Ordinary buttons, notebooks, captions, clamps and static sensing are legitimate comparison arms that can win; they do not delete later silent/cooperative or physical ambitions.
