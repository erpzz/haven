### Concrete conflicts and provisional resolutions

1. **Time units:** R02 proposes integer milliseconds; R06/R08 use seconds and source ticks. Retain native values, unit, precision and clock epoch in domain records; require explicit validated seconds↔milliseconds adapter with stated rounding and overflow handling. Source tick↔UTC requires a clock mapping and uncertainty; do not compare monotonic clocks across boots/hosts. No owner agreement is asserted yet.
2. **Geometry:** R08 CAD millimetres convert explicitly to H08 metre frames; retain datum, pose and calibration. R06 specifies parent-frame left perturbation and covariance order `[tx,ty,tz,rx,ry,rz]`; R08 names the order but needs confirmation of the same perturbation convention. Reject unresolved frame/covariance mapping. A nominal CAD pose remains predicted/assumed, not measured.
3. **Scope:** R06 public licensed research cannot be relabeled synthetic merely to fit a v1 owner enum. I01 must carry reviewed data class and subject/area/session scope. Person identification cannot be inferred from anonymous tracks or RF estimates.
4. **Ordering:** One revision-vector vocabulary can be shared, but publication, digital writes and physical dispatch retain their own authoritative ordering points and post-release/revocation limitations. This is a proposed design choice pending R03, not an implemented atomicity guarantee.
5. **Cancellation:** Suppressed/withheld output is not proof computation stopped. EVIDENCE-0 E0-H1 remains an independent R1 gap. I04 retains observed termination versus unknown and does not let a cancelled job restore budget as if no charge occurred.
6. **Evidence and acceptance:** Exact quote matching is a narrow N1 behavior, not semantic model grounding. A fixed N2 arithmetic checker and retained failures do not constitute independent metrology. Model, spatial and engineering qualifications retain separate scope and reviewer authority.
