# R15C — Public sources, open tools, offline packs and operating cost

Design the smallest sustainable data/tool ecosystem for Haven Observatory. Free/open source is a selection preference, not permission to ignore data rights, service quotas or maintenance.

## Inputs

Read the full R15 prompt and `coordination/R15_OBSERVATORY.md` at a recorded https://github.com/erpzz/haven commit, the supporting SOURCE_NOTES.md and issue #12. Inspect https://github.com/erpzz/gods-eye-view at a separately recorded fork commit; initial reference `81eb44340d90feda5b5283438f6e5fdad5cabbdd`. Read the actual LICENSE, DATA_SOURCES.md, third-party notices, relevant provider modules and any R15A output.

## Work

Compare 8–12 relevant tools/source routes across maps, environmental observations, transport, imagery, sensor interchange and replay. Start from the master prompt's candidates, then narrow. For each separate software license, bundled assets, data license, service terms, API/key/account requirement, free tier, billing exposure, storage/egress, offline/caching/redistribution rights, attribution, refresh/history availability and maintenance. Verify current primary documentation; record inaccessible terms and unresolved eligibility rather than guessing.

Propose an account-free/no-paid route, a free-key route and a separately optional capped paid route. At least one first-demo route needs no billing credential. Use a declared workload: area, layer count, requests, imagery volume, update rate, retained history, simultaneous users and local energy/storage assumptions. Do not represent free-tier capacity as a guaranteed emergency service or a budget alert as a hard stop.

For public-source examples compare one regional environmental use case, not a precise household coordinate. Preserve coarse spatial resolution, source age and distinct capture/receive/publication times. No broad scraping or bulk datasets are needed for this research. A missing point is not proof of no event; public map features are not current observations of people.

## Return and boundaries

Proposed output `design/H00/R15/inputs/R15C/v1/`: SOURCES_MATRIX.md, COST_MODEL.md, OFFLINE_PLAN.md, SOURCES.md and SUMMARY.md. Recommend a small first source set, acquisition/retention rules, expected outage behavior and one finite later adapter test. H04 owns egress/privacy, H11 communications and H08 geospatial semantics; flag their decisions.

Do not create accounts, acquire keys, download bulk tiles/data, make paid calls, connect sensors, change either repo, install software or start monitoring. Public documentation research only. Prices are dated observations or explicitly labeled assumptions; no hardware purchase is authorized.
