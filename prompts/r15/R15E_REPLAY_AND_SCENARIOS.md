# R15E — Evidence replay, scenario forks and spatial/engineering continuity

Design an inspectable temporal and scenario model for Haven Observatory. Its purpose is to understand recorded information and compare explicit hypotheses, not generate convincing scenes that masquerade as measurements.

## Inputs

At a recorded https://github.com/erpzz/haven commit read root instructions, `coordination/R15_OBSERVATORY.md`, the full R15 prompt, issue #12 and the opportunity brief. Read the available R06 spatial-semantics proposals, R07 world-model decisions, R02 context/cancellation contracts and R08 engineering lineage at their actual paths/versions. Missing decisions remain provisional. Inspect GEV Director/share-restoration/annotation and relevant source/time behavior in the actual fork; record exact commit and files.

## Work

Define event, capture, provider-publication, receipt, correction and model-derivation times. Separate 'what did we know then?' from corrected historical reconstruction. Show how revoked/deleted material affects future replay without promising to retract already displayed information. Track source revisions, transformations, frame/altitude references, uncertainty, incomplete history, correlations and retention rights.

A scenario fork must have immutable input references, explicit changed assumptions, model/solver version, finite resource limits, synthetic output identity and no live executor credentials or endpoint path. Compare two narrow scenarios such as a lost owned relay and alternative sensor placement. Start with deterministic rules/geometry and choose a more complex simulator only where the question needs it. Mode switches, copy/paste, scene import and cancellation must not leak simulated state into live records.

Connect scene observations to instruments, calibration, fixture/CAD revisions and experiments without asserting unmeasured geometric registration. Define meaningful uncertainty/support visualization and explain why a modeled safe path is not an actual safety certificate. Reuse domain artifacts; do not create a competing evidence database or engineering coordinator.

## Return and boundaries

Proposed output `design/H00/R15/inputs/R15E/v1/`: TEMPORAL_MODEL.md, SCENARIO_DESIGN.md, CROSS_DOMAIN_LINEAGE.md and SUMMARY.md. Include two finite experiment protocols, exact expected outputs, negative cases, simple baselines and integration questions for H04/H08/H09/H06.

No code/simulator runs, map acquisition, real location data, radio measurements, device movement, installation, paid APIs or repo writes. Public research and design only. Mark predictions/hypotheses and all proposed tests honestly. Stop before implementation.
