## Product intent

Haven Observatory is a spatial workspace: inspect the world, replay retained observations, expose uncertainty, compare qualified resources, fork isolated scenarios and connect observations to engineering experiments. It expands Haven beyond personal assistance without deleting the personal assistant, independently private users, wearables, intentional communication, RF research, drones, robotics, emergency communications or fabrication.

God/admin POV describes the experience of understanding and coordinating one's authorized systems. It is not unrestricted surveillance or authority over other people. Map navigation, analysis, a simulated change, an action proposal and observed physical execution remain different operations.

## How to use the research threads

Start one R15 master research chat using the main assignment. It may complete the integrated study itself. R15A–F are optional decomposition, not six prerequisites or six automatically running agents. Split only when useful; start no more than two or three at once. If using specialists, A/B/C can investigate independently; D/E reconcile their findings and existing owner decisions; F reviews the integrated result. No specialist changes another thread's files or finalizes shared contracts alone.

The main research result belongs in a future, unused `design/H00/R15/v1/` directory. The existing `brief-v1/` directory contains assignment inputs, not a completed handback. Specialist outputs have separate proposed directories listed in their prompts. Research creation and later publication are separate instructions; a thread without write authorization returns files marked NOT_UPLOADED.

Use issue #12 for concise questions and links. Every response identifies its baseline commit, artifact path, exact decision, required owner, assumptions and what can proceed independently. Issues do not wake stopped chats. No bot, scheduled task or notification system is installed here.
